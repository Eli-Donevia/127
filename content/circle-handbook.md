# The Founder's Circle handbook

For members. Read it before your first call; it takes about 10 minutes.

---

## Welcome to the table

You hold one of four seats. Every week, four founders and Eli sit at one table. You bring your number and your hardest problem. You leave with a decision, a next move, and three people who will ask you about it next week.

Think of it as a small board for your business, with Eli in the chair. It isn't a course, a chat group or one-to-one consulting. It works when you show up, tell the truth about your numbers, and do what you said you'd do.

## Your first 7 days

- [ ] **Fill in your Founder Profile** (10 minutes): what you're building, your North Number, and your first Build Target.
- [ ] **Join the Circle's Discord** from the invite in your welcome email. Read `#start-here`.
- [ ] **Post your intro** in `#the-circle`: who you are, what you're building, your number today, and one thing you're great at that the others could use.
- [ ] **Send your first Scorecard** before your first call.
- [ ] **Take your seat.** Your first call opens with 3 minutes for you: who you are, what you're building, your number, your Build Target. Eli names your Build Partner at the end.

Your first call is always within 7 days of joining.

## How a week works

| When | What | Your time |
|---|---|---|
| Call night, next morning | **The recap** lands in `#call-notes` and your inbox: decisions, your commitments, next week's hot seats | 5 min |
| Midweek | **Build Partner check-in**, 15 minutes, voice or video | 15 min |
| Any day | **The group** in Discord: wins, questions for each other | as you like |
| 2 days before the call | **Your Scorecard opens** | |
| Midnight ET, the night before the call | **Scorecard due** | 3 min |
| **Thursdays, 8pm ET** | **The call**, 45 minutes, in The Room | 45 min |

Plus 2-3 hours of real work on your business between calls. That's where the results come from.

Holiday weeks (Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve) move the call to **Tuesday of that week, same time.** Any other change is posted in `#start-here` at least a week ahead.

## The weekly call

| Minute | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0-2 | **Open.** Eli's recording notice. An optional 30-second opening (a verse or a short prayer; you can always pass). Then **the Bell**, if someone hit a milestone. |
| 2-10 | **Scorecards.** 2 minutes each: your commitments (done or not done), your number, what's in the way. |
| 10-23 | **Hot seat 1.** 13 minutes on one member's real decision. |
| 23-36 | **Hot seat 2.** Same, for a second member. |
| 36-41 | **Rapid round.** The two members not in a hot seat each get one quick question answered. |
| 41-45 | **Commitments.** One or two each for next week: "By next call I will..." |
| 45-60 | **Open table**, optional. Stay and keep talking. Not recorded. |

**Getting a hot seat.** With four members, you get one every other week. Ask for it in your Scorecard: "I need it", "If there's room" or "Not this week". If something can't wait, mark it time-sensitive. If you give your seat up for someone with a deadline, you go first next week.

**How a hot seat runs (13 minutes):**
1. **2 minutes:** you give the situation and the decision you need to make.
2. **3 minutes:** questions only. The others ask first; Eli asks last. No advice yet.
3. **3 minutes:** each peer says what they'd do if it were theirs, 1 minute each.
4. **4 minutes:** Eli's call: what he'd do, why, and what he'd watch for.
5. **1 minute:** you say what you'll do this week. That's your commitment.

**Being a good peer.** Ask questions that make the decision clearer. Give your real take, not a polite one. Share what worked for you, not what you read somewhere. Keep it about the idea, never the person.

## Your Scorecard

Three minutes on your phone, due **midnight ET the night before the call.** It replaces small talk on the call and tells Eli where to spend the time.

1. **Last call you said you'd do this:** Done, Partly or Not done for each commitment, and one line on what you learned.
2. **Your number** (your North Number), with your last 4 weeks shown. Share it with the group, or with Eli only.
3. **Win of the week.** Tick **Ring the Bell** for a milestone: first sale, first $1k month, first hire, business registered, launched.
4. **What's in the way?** One line.
5. **Do you need a hot seat?** If yes: the situation, what you've tried, what you'd do if you had to decide today.
6. **One quick question** for the rapid round.
7. **Will you be on the call?**
8. **Anything Eli should know first?** Private to Eli.

Late Scorecards still count, marked late.

## Your Build Target and North Number

The Circle runs in **Build Blocks** of 13 weeks. At the start of each block you set one **Build Target**: one result you control, that you can measure, and that you can reach in 13 weeks.

Good targets: "10 paying customers." "$1,000 in sales in one month." "Launch and get 25 orders." "Hire my first contractor." Not good: "go viral", "get rich", "grow my brand".

Your **North Number** is the one number you report every week because it moves your target.

| Where you are | Good North Numbers |
|---|---|
| Not selling yet | Customer conversations this week · pre-orders or deposits · people on the waitlist |
| First sales | Paying customers to date · sales this week ($) · orders this week |
| Growing | Revenue, last 4 weeks ($) · qualified leads this week · conversion rate (%) |

Bad North Numbers: followers, hours worked, anything that can't move in a week, anything someone else controls.

**Week 13 is the Reset call.** 5 minutes each: what you aimed for, what happened, what you learned, your next target. Afterwards Eli writes you a personal **Founder Letter** about your block, and you answer a 3-question survey.

Joined mid-block? Set a target for the weeks that are left.

## Between calls

- **The group (Discord).** `#start-here` has the Charter, the schedule and a backup call link. `#the-circle` is for wins, questions and help for each other. `#call-notes` holds every recap. `#48-hour-asks` is for your monthly ask (below). The weekly call happens in the voice channel **The Room**.
- **Build Partners.** You're paired with one member for each block. 15 minutes midweek, three questions: did you do what you said, what's next, what's in the way. Use a Partner Room in Discord or any video app.
- **Your 48-hour ask.** Once a month you can post one time-sensitive question in `#48-hour-asks`: a deal, a hire, a launch decision. Eli answers in the group within 48 hours on weekdays, by voice note or text, so all four learn from it.
- **Eli's Playbook.** Your plan includes 600 messages a month. It's the right place for "how do I..." questions between calls.
- **Everything in Builder:** the full library of playbooks, templates and AI tools.

**What doesn't happen:** DMs with Eli, one-to-one calls, or reviews of your deck or site outside the call. Bring those to a hot seat. That's how Eli keeps showing up for all four of you every week, and every answer helps four people instead of one.

## Founder Days

Twice a year the Circle meets in person for a day and a half: **Friday 2pm to Saturday 4pm.**

- **Friday:** a board meeting for each member (30 minutes, the group as your board), then dinner together.
- **Saturday:** field work in the morning (customer conversations, or a visit to a local founder), lunch, then your 6-month plan and a close.
- **Dates** are announced at the Reset calls, at least 60 days ahead. Spring is in Eli's home city; fall is wherever is easiest for the group to reach.
- **Who pays:** 127 pays the meeting space, Friday dinner, Saturday lunch and materials. You pay your own travel, lodging and other meals. 127 doesn't buy alcohol.
- **Can't travel?** Join the Saturday planning hour by video.

## The Charter

The 10 rules of the room. By taking a seat, you agree to them.

1. **Show up live, on time, camera on.** Tell the group before you miss.
2. **Scorecard in by midnight ET before every call.**
3. **Real numbers only.** Small numbers are welcome; made-up ones aren't.
4. **Done or not done, then what you learned.** No stories.
5. **Questions before advice.**
6. **Give before you get.** Help someone else every week.
7. **What's said here stays here.** Say "off the record" and it stays out of the notes.
8. **No selling to each other, no recruiting.** If two of you want to do business, take it outside the Circle.
9. **Eli answers on the call, in the recap, or in a 48-hour ask.** Not in DMs.
10. **Faith is Eli's foundation.** You don't have to share it to belong here.

## Recording and privacy

- Calls are transcribed by Granola so the recap can be written. Eli says so at the start of every call. There's no video recording.
- Say **"off the record"** and Eli pauses the notes. The recap only says that one part was left out at a member's request.
- The open table after the call is never recorded.
- Raw transcripts are deleted after 90 days. Recaps are kept while the Circle runs, and you have your own copy by email.
- Your private note to Eli and any number you mark "Eli only" never reach the group.
- The Circle agreement and the Privacy Policy have the full terms.

## If you miss a call

- Tell the group in `#the-circle` before the call.
- Send your Scorecard anyway. Your quick question gets answered in the recap.
- The recap is your catch-up. Read it before your Build Partner check-in.
- Miss two in a row and Eli will check in. A seat that isn't used costs the other three, so if your season has changed, say so. Switching to Builder is always an option.

**If Eli can't make a call,** it moves to the next evening at the same time. If that doesn't work, you run a 20-minute peer call (Scorecards and commitments) and Eli answers the hot-seat questions in writing in the recap. The Circle never skips a week without a plan.

## Leaving, and coming back

- **Cancel online anytime from Billing.** No call or email needed. Your access runs to the end of the month you've paid for.
- On the way out you can switch to Builder instead, or book an optional 15-minute call with Eli. The call is never required.
- If you'd like, your last call opens with a 2-minute goodbye.
- Former members keep a Circle alumni mark in the app and can come back within 6 months without re-applying, when a seat is open.

## Quick answers

**What if I'm not on track?** That's what the room is for. "Not done" plus what you learned is a good Scorecard.

**Can I bring a cofounder?** One seat, one person. If your cofounder wants a seat, they apply.

**Is this financial, legal or tax advice?** No. Eli shares how he'd think about a decision as a founder. For legal, tax or investment questions, talk to a licensed professional.

**Does the Circle promise results?** No. You get the room, the structure and Eli's honest take every week. The results come from the work you do between calls.

**Who do I ask about billing or the app?** Reply to any 127 email, or use Help in the app.
