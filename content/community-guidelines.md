
**127 is a room of founders building with purpose.** Most of us are early. Everyone here is 18 or older. All of us are here to do the work. These rules keep this a place you'd want to post your first win.

## Build each other up
- Post real progress: a step done, a conversation, a first sale, a lesson from a "no".
- Cheer for other people's wins. No one is self-made.
- Disagree with ideas, never with people. Be kind first, honest second, and never cruel.

## Keep it real
- Share what actually happened. Small numbers are welcome here. Made-up numbers are not.
- No income promises, no "this system made me $10k a month", no get-rich anything.
- Your faith is welcome. So is your neighbor's. Nobody has to share Eli's to belong here.

## Keep it safe
- No phone numbers, emails, home addresses or "DM me on another app" in posts. To keep members' details private and scams out, contact details and off-platform invitations wait for a quick check before they go up.
- Every member is 18 or older, and so is everyone at our in-person gatherings. You must be 21 or older to drink there, and 127 doesn't buy alcohol.
- No hate, harassment, sexual content or threats. Ever.
- If a post is about someone being hurt or wanting to hurt themselves, we read it right away. If that's you, call or text 988 (US) any time. If you're in danger now, call 911.

## No selling here
- The wins board is for wins, not ads. A link to the thing you launched is fine once you've been here two weeks. A pitch, a discount code or "link in bio" is not.
- No crypto, forex, MLM or "investment opportunity" posts.

## What happens if something's off
Most posts go up right away. Some wait for a quick check by a person, usually within a day. If a post breaks these rules:
1. It comes down, and we tell you why.
2. If it happens again, posting is paused for 7 days.
3. If it keeps happening, or it's serious the first time (hate, threats, anything that puts a minor at risk), Eli closes the account and cancels any subscription.

## Report something
Tap **Report** on any post. Two reports take a post down until a person looks at it. A safety report takes it down right away. For anything urgent or private, use **Help** in the app or email hello@127group.io.

Using 127 means you agree to these guidelines and our [Terms](/terms).
