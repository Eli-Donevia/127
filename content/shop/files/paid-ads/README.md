# Run paid ads

How to tell whether you're ready for ads, run a small first test, make creative people stop for, check your landing page, read the numbers, and decide each day what to keep, cut or grow. Meta (Instagram and Facebook) first, with a lesson on Google search.

1. Check you're ready, and find your break-even number (01).
2. Set up a small first test (02).
3. Make the creative (03).
4. Check the page the ad sends people to (04).
5. Read the numbers and make the daily call (05).
6. Try Google search when people already look for what you sell (06).
7. Stay inside the rules (07).

Ads spend real money. Start with an amount you're fine losing while you learn, and never borrow to fund a test. Nothing here promises sales or results.

Each lesson ends with a prompt you can paste into any AI with your details filled in. Inside 127 HQ the matching workflows do the same job with your business facts already loaded.
