# Check the page the ad sends people to

An ad that gets clicks and no sales usually has a page problem. Check these on your own phone, on mobile data, before you spend.

## The checklist

- [ ] **It matches the ad.** The first line on the page repeats the ad's promise. Someone who tapped "custom dog portraits" should see dog portraits, not your homepage.
- [ ] **It loads in about two seconds** on mobile data. Big images are the usual cause; compress them.
- [ ] **The price is visible** without hunting.
- [ ] **One button**, repeated. Not five links to other pages.
- [ ] **Proof near the button**: a review, a photo of real work, a refund policy you actually keep.
- [ ] **Checkout works on a phone**, with Apple Pay or Google Pay if you can.
- [ ] **The thank-you page fires your tracking event.**

## The two-second test

Show the page to someone for two seconds, then hide it. Ask: what is it, and what does it cost? If they can't say both, rewrite the top of the page.

## Prompt: check my page

```text
Here's my ad: [hook and caption]. Here's my landing page: [paste the text, top to bottom, and describe the images].

Check the page against this list: matches the ad's promise in the first line; price easy to find; one main button; proof near the button; nothing that pulls people away. Give me the three changes most likely to get more people from click to purchase, in order, and rewrite the first screen.
```
