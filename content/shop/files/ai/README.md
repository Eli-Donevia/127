# 127 AI Pack

53 prompts for running a small business with AI, grouped by job, plus the operator instructions that make any AI
start from your business instead of from zero.

1. Open 00-operator-instructions.md and paste it into a Claude Project (or ChatGPT custom GPT, or Gemini Gem) once.
2. Pick the file for the job in front of you. Fill in the {{blanks}}, paste, and send.
3. Paste the answer back in and ask for the next step. Each prompt ends with what to do next.

- 00-operator-instructions.md: Operator instructions (paste once into a Claude Project or ChatGPT custom instructions)
- 01-start.md: Start
- 02-brain.md: Business brain
- 03-offer.md: Offer
- 04-content.md: Content and hooks
- 05-video.md: Video
- 06-website.md: Website and store
- 07-finance.md: Finance and forecasting
- 08-ops.md: Ops and SOPs
- 09-review.md: Review

These prompts are yours to use in your own business and for your clients. Please don't resell or share the pack itself.
