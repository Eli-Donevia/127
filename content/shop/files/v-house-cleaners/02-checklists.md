# Room-by-room checklists

Checklists for standard, deep and move-out cleans your team can follow.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write room-by-room checklists for [business]: kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, entry. Three levels: standard, deep, move-out (what's added at each). Short lines a cleaner can check off on a phone.
```

## How to use it

Print one per job, or keep them in your jobs app.
