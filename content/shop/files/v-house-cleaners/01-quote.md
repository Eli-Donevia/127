# Quote by home size

A price list by bedrooms, bathrooms and extras, from your time per room and target hourly rate.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Build a house cleaning price list. My target per hour [$] per cleaner. Time: per bedroom [min], per bathroom [min], kitchen [min], extras (fridge, oven, windows, baseboards) [min each], drive time average [min]. Give standard, deep and move-out prices for 1 to 4 bedrooms, the extras list, and a text I send with a quote.
```

## How to use it

Quote in the first reply. People book the first clear price.
