# Recurring schedule texts

Reminder, running late, reschedule and after-clean texts for recurring homes.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write short texts for [business]: the day-before reminder, running late, need to reschedule, after the clean (with a photo note), and the one-month check-in for a recurring home. Under 30 words each, with STOP to opt out.
```

## How to use it

Save them as text shortcuts.
