# Recurring schedule texts

Reminder, running late, reschedule and after-clean texts for recurring homes.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write short texts for [business]: the day-before reminder, running late, need to reschedule, after the clean (with a photo note), and the one-month check-in for a recurring home. Under 30 words each. Each text names [business] and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out." Say the price, how often it charges and how to cancel, and make cancelling as easy as signing up.
```

## How to use it

Save them as text shortcuts.

## Before you send

- Text only customers who gave you their number for texts (most booking apps ask at booking). Each one names your business and has STOP. Send between 8am and 8pm their time, one at a time or from your booking app, never to a list you bought or copied.
- Before anyone signs up, the plan says the price, how often it charges and how to cancel. Cancelling is as easy as signing up.
