# Service agreement outline

An outline of what your dispatch agreement should cover, to take to a lawyer.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Outline a dispatch service agreement between [my business] and a carrier. Cover: what I do and don't do, my fee and when it's paid, who signs rate cons, the carrier's right to refuse a load, how either side ends it, and what records I keep. Mark it as an outline for a lawyer to review, not a contract.
```

## How to use it

Have a lawyer turn it into your agreement before you sign carriers.
