# Weekly puppy update

Turns your weigh-in and notes into a post and an email for the families waiting.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write this week's puppy update for [kennel]. Litter: [parents, born date]. Notes per puppy: [collar color, weight, what they did this week]. Write a short email to the waitlist families (warm, specific, one photo note per puppy) and a post caption (no prices, no pressure). Never promise a puppy's future size, health or temperament.
```

## How to use it

Send every Monday after weigh-in. Families share these.
