# Buyer questionnaire

A questionnaire for families on the waitlist, written in your words.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a buyer questionnaire for [kennel], a [breed] breeder. I want to know: home and yard, work schedule, other pets, kids, experience with the breed, who the main caretaker is, vet they'll use, and what they'd do if they couldn't keep the dog. Friendly, 12 questions at most, with a note that I return every family's call.
```

## How to use it

Link it from your waitlist page.
