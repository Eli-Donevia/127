Made by scripts/build-verticals.js from content/verticals/dog-breeders.json. Don't edit these files; edit the vertical and build again.

- Weekly puppy update
- Buyer questionnaire
- Go-home packet

## Before you use these

- These files assume families meet the puppy in person. If you have more than four breeding females and sell sight unseen (shipped, or sold online without meeting), you need a USDA license (9 CFR 1.1 and 2.1). Your state may also need a license, health certificates and lemon law terms.
- Post from a business profile that shows your kennel's name and where to find you. Prices, deposits and "reserve" or "DM to claim" stay on your site, never in posts: Meta removes person-to-person animal sales.
- Saved replies about deposits and health testing use only your own terms. Never let an AI write a health guarantee, refund or deposit rule you didn't give it.
