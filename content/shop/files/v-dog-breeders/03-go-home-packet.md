# Go-home packet

A packet for the day a puppy goes home: food, schedule, first week and who to call.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a go-home packet for a [breed] puppy from [kennel] going home at [weeks] weeks. Include: the food and amounts we use [ ], the daily schedule, crate and potty tips for the first week, what the puppy has had from our vet [ ] and what's next (ask your vet), what's in the bag, and my number. Tell families to follow their own vet's advice.
```

## How to use it

Print it and email it the same day.

From the Breeder Pack by 127. Use it in your own business and for your clients; please don't resell the pack.
