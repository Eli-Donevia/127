# Before-and-after reel

A 15-second reel brief built around the reveal.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a 15-second before-and-after reel brief for a [vehicle] at [business]. Plan the first second (the worst spot), three working shots, the reveal with the sound moment, on-screen words (six at most) and the caption with the package and price.
```

## How to use it

Film every job's worst spot first, before you touch it.
