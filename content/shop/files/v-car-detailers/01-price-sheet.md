# Price by size and condition

Package prices for sedans, SUVs and trucks, with condition upcharges.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Build detailing prices. Target per hour [$]. Hours for interior, exterior and full detail on a sedan [ ], add [%] for SUVs and [%] for trucks or vans. Condition upcharges: pet hair, heavy stains, sand [ ]. Add-ons: [ ]. Give a price menu and the line I use when someone asks why a dirty car costs more.
```

## How to use it

Ask for two photos before you quote.
