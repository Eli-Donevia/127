# Monthly maintenance plan

A monthly plan offer and the text that offers it after a full detail.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a monthly maintenance plan for [business]: what's included each visit and the price [ ]. Then a short text to send the day after a full detail offering it. Say the price, how often it charges and how to cancel, and make cancelling as easy as signing up. Each text names [business] and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
```

## How to use it

Offer it to every full detail.

## Before you send

- Before anyone signs up, the plan says the price, how often it charges and how to cancel. Cancelling is as easy as signing up.
- Text only customers who gave you their number for texts (most booking apps ask at booking). Each one names your business and has STOP. Send between 8am and 8pm their time, one at a time or from your booking app, never to a list you bought or copied.
