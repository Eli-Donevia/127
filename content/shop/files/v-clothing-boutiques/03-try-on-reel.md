# Try-on reel brief

One outfit styled three ways, as a 20-second reel with the first second and the shot order.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a 20-second try-on reel brief for the [piece] from [boutique]. Style it three ways: [occasions]. Include the first second (the piece moving, not a title card), the shot order, where to stand for light, on-screen words (five per look at most), and the caption with the price and sizes left.
```

## How to use it

Film it in one take per look in the fitting room. Post the day the piece goes live.
