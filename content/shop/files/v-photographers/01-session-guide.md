# Session guide

A guide clients read before the shoot: what to wear, where, when and what to expect.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a session guide for a [session type] with [my business] at [location] around [time]. Cover: what to wear (colors, textures, what to skip), what to bring, how kids or pets usually go, the weather plan, how long it takes, and when photos arrive. Warm and short.
```

## How to use it

Send it when they book and again two days before.
