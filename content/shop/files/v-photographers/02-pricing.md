# Session pricing

Prices a session from shoot, travel, culling and editing hours.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Price a [session type]. Shoot [hours], travel [hours], culling [hours], editing [hours], gallery and delivery [hours]. Costs per session: [ ]. Target per hour: [$]. Show the price, then three packages, and the line I use when someone asks why.
```

## How to use it

Re-run when your editing time changes.
