# Chair math

Compares chair rent and commission at your price and cuts per day.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Compare chair rent [$ per week] vs commission [% to me] at [cuts per day], [days per week], average ticket [$], product sales [$]. Show my take-home both ways, and the cuts per week where they break even.
```

## How to use it

Run it before you sign a chair deal.
