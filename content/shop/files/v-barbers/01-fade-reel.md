# Fade reel

A 15-second reel brief for one cut, with the chair spin reveal.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a 15-second reel brief for a [cut] at [shop]. The first second, three working shots, the chair-spin reveal, on-screen words (five at most), and the caption with the cut's name and price. Only post the client with their OK.
```

## How to use it

Ask before you film. Most say yes.
