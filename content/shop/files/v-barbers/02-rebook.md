# Rebook texts

Texts at two, three and four weeks, by the cut.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write three short rebook texts for [barber] at [shop]: two weeks (fades), three weeks (regular cuts), four weeks (everyone else). Under 25 words, booking link [link]. Each text names [business] and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
```

## How to use it

Send from your booking app.

## Before you send

- Text only customers who gave you their number for texts (most booking apps ask at booking). Each one names your business and has STOP. Send between 8am and 8pm their time, one at a time or from your booking app, never to a list you bought or copied.
