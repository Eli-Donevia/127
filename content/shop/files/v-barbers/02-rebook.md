# Rebook texts

Texts at two, three and four weeks, by the cut.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write three short rebook texts for [barber] at [shop]: two weeks (fades), three weeks (regular cuts), four weeks (everyone else). Under 25 words, booking link [link], STOP to opt out.
```
