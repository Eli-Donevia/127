# The product page

One page, built for a phone, that answers the questions a buyer has in the order they have them.

## Top to bottom

1. **What it is, in one line.** "A Notion client portal for wedding photographers."
2. **What you'll have after using it.** One sentence, ending in a noun.
3. **Buy button, with the price on it.** "Get it: $29". Repeat it at the bottom, and keep it on screen on phones.
4. **A look inside.** Real screenshots or a 15-second screen recording. This does more than any paragraph.
5. **What's inside, as a list.** Real counts: "12 templates", "30 prompts", "5 lessons". Name each one.
6. **Who it's for, and who it isn't.** Two short lists.
7. **How delivery works.** "You get a link by email right after you pay. Files are yours to keep."
8. **The licence.** "Use it in your own business and for your clients. Don't resell or share the files."
9. **Refunds.** One sentence, the real policy.
10. **Questions.** The three you get asked most, answered in a sentence each.

## Write it plainly

- Say what it does, not how it makes them feel.
- Cut every word that would fit on any product page ("ultimate", "game-changing", "unlock").
- If you mention results (yours or a buyer's), make them real and add: "One person's real numbers. Yours will differ. No results promised."

## Prompt: write your page

```text
Write a product page for [product] for [who]. Price: [$]. What's inside: [list with counts]. Delivery: [how]. Licence: [terms]. Refunds: [policy]. Common questions: [list].

Follow this order: one-line what it is; what you'll have after using it; buy button with the price; a look inside (describe the screenshots I should take); what's inside as a named list; who it's for and who it isn't; how delivery works; licence; refunds; three questions and answers.

Plain words. No "ultimate", "unlock", "game-changing", no results promises, no fake discounts.
```
