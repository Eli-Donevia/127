# Checkout, delivery and refunds

Pick the simplest setup that gets money in and files out automatically. You can move later.

## Three ways to sell

| Setup | Good for | You handle |
|---|---|---|
| A marketplace or digital storefront (Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, Etsy for printables) | Your first product, this week | Almost nothing: the platform runs checkout, delivery and often sales tax. Higher fees. |
| Stripe Payment Links or Checkout on your own site | When you have a site and want lower fees | Delivery email or download page, receipts, sales tax settings. |
| Shopify with a digital downloads app | When you already sell physical products there | The app setup. |

Some platforms act as the "merchant of record", which means they collect and pay sales tax and VAT for you. That's worth a lot when you sell worldwide. Check before you choose.

## Delivery

- **Right away, automatically.** Nobody should wait for you to wake up.
- **One email with one link**, plus the same link on the thank-you page.
- **A "Start here" file first** in whatever they download.
- **A way back in.** People lose emails. Let them get their files again with the email they paid with.

## The licence, in plain words

"You can use this in your own business and for your clients. You can't resell it, share the files, or include them in another product." Put it on the product page, in the delivery email and in the "Start here" file.

## Refunds

Decide before your first sale and write it on the page. A common choice for digital products is a short refund window (7 to 14 days) for anyone who's unhappy, no questions asked. It costs a few refunds and earns a lot of trust. Whatever you choose, follow it every time.

## Before you announce it

- [ ] Buy your own product with a real card, then refund yourself.
- [ ] Open the delivery email on your phone.
- [ ] Open every file on your phone.
- [ ] Check the receipt shows your business name.

## Prompt: write the delivery email

```text
Write the delivery email for [product]. It should: thank them in one line, give the one link to their files, say to open "Start here" first, state the licence in one sentence, explain how to get the files again if they lose this email, and say how to reach me. Under 120 words, plain and warm.
```
