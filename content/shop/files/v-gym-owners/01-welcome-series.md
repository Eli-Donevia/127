# New member welcome series

Five short emails for a new member's first two weeks.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a five-email welcome series for new members at [gym]. Day 0: welcome and what to bring. Day 2: how the first class goes. Day 5: meet the coaches [names, one line each]. Day 9: the schedule and how to book. Day 14: check in, ask how it's going. Each under 120 words, signed by [owner]. No promises about weight loss or results.
```

## How to use it

Set it up once in your email tool and let it run.
