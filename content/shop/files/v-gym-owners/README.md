Made by scripts/build-verticals.js from content/verticals/gym-owners.json. Don't edit these files; edit the vertical and build again.

## Before you use these

- Kids on camera only with a parent's OK. Members on camera only with their OK.
- The challenge rewards everyone who meets the attendance bar the same way: no drawing, ranking or winner's prize on a paid entry.
- Monthly memberships say the price, how often it charges and how to cancel, and follow your state's health club law. Quiet-member and win-back messages never stand in the way of a cancel.
