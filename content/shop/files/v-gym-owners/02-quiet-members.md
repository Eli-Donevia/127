A personal text for a member who hasn't been in for two weeks.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write three versions of a short text from [coach] at [gym] to a member who hasn't checked in for two weeks. Warm, no guilt, one question, offer a class time [ ]. Under 30 words each, with a way to opt out.
```
