# Quiet member check-in

A personal text for a member who hasn't been in for two weeks.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write three versions of a short text from [coach] at [gym] to a member who hasn't checked in for two weeks. Warm, no guilt, one question, offer a class time [ ]. Under 30 words each. Nothing in them makes cancelling harder. Each text names [business] and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
```

## How to use it

Send them from your phone, one by one, every Thursday.

## Before you send

- Text only customers who gave you their number for texts (most booking apps ask at booking). Each one names your business and has STOP. Send between 8am and 8pm their time, one at a time or from your booking app, never to a list you bought or copied.
