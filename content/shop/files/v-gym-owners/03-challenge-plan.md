# Six-week challenge plan

Plans an in-gym challenge: the rules, the weekly posts, the check-ins and the finale.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Plan a six-week in-gym challenge at [gym] for [members or newcomers]. Price [ ]. Include: rules and what counts (attendance, not body weight), the weekly post, a weekly check-in message, the finale day, and how we'll celebrate. Everyone who meets the attendance bar gets the same reward: no drawing, ranking or winner's prize. No before-and-after body photos, no weight-loss claims, and kids on camera only with a parent's OK.
```

## How to use it

Run it twice a year. Count attendance, not pounds.
