# Rain day texts

Texts for a rain delay, a reschedule and a skipped week.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write short texts for [business]: rain delay today (new day [ ]), we're running a day behind all week, and skipping this week because of drought or heat. Under 30 words. Each text names [business] and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
```

## How to use it

Send them to the whole route from your phone or app.

## Before you send

- Text only customers who gave you their number for texts (most booking apps ask at booking). Each one names your business and has STOP. Send between 8am and 8pm their time, one at a time or from your booking app, never to a list you bought or copied.
