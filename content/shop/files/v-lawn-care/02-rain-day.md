# Rain day texts

Texts for a rain delay, a reschedule and a skipped week.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write short texts for [business]: rain delay today (new day [ ]), we're running a day behind all week, and skipping this week because of drought or heat. Under 30 words, with STOP to opt out.
```
