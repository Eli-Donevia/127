# Rebook texts

Rebook texts at four, six and eight weeks, by coat type.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write three short rebook texts for [shop]: one at four weeks for short coats, one at six for doodles and curly coats, one at eight for everyone else. Each under 30 words, with the booking link [link]. Each text names [business] and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
```

## How to use it

Send from your booking app or phone.

## Before you send

- Text only customers who gave you their number for texts (most booking apps ask at booking). Each one names your business and has STOP. Send between 8am and 8pm their time, one at a time or from your booking app, never to a list you bought or copied.
