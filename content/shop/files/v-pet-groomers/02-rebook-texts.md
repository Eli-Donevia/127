# Rebook texts

Rebook texts at four, six and eight weeks, by coat type.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write three short rebook texts for [shop]: one at four weeks for short coats, one at six for doodles and curly coats, one at eight for everyone else. Each under 30 words, with the booking link [link], and a way to reply STOP.
```

## How to use it

Send from your booking app or phone.
