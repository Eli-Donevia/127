# Price by size and coat

Builds your grooming price list from your time on the table and the price per hour you want.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Help me set grooming prices for [shop]. My target per hour: [$]. Typical table time: small [min], medium [min], large [min], extra for matting [min per level], doodle coat [min]. Build a price list by size and service, with a matting fee, and a line for how I explain it to an owner.
```

## How to use it

Print the list for the front desk.
