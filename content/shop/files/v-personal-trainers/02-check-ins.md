# Weekly check-ins

Short weekly texts for week one, the middle of a package and the last two sessions.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write short check-in texts from [trainer] for: the first week, the middle of a package, two sessions left (with the renewal option), and after a missed session. Under 35 words each, warm, one question.
```
