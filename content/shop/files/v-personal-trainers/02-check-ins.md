# Weekly check-ins

Short weekly texts for week one, the middle of a package and the last two sessions.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write short check-in texts from [trainer] for: the first week, the middle of a package, two sessions left (with the renewal option), and after a missed session. Under 35 words each, warm, one question. Each text names [business] and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out." Say the price, how often it charges and how to cancel, and make cancelling as easy as signing up.
```

## How to use it

Send Tuesdays.

## Before you send

- Text only customers who gave you their number for texts (most booking apps ask at booking). Each one names your business and has STOP. Send between 8am and 8pm their time, one at a time or from your booking app, never to a list you bought or copied.
- Before anyone signs up, the plan says the price, how often it charges and how to cancel. Cancelling is as easy as signing up.
