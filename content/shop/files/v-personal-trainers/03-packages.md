# Session packages

Builds three packages from your hourly rate, with the renewal price.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Build three personal training packages. My rate per session [$], sessions per week options [ ], package lengths [ ]. Give names, prices, what's included, a renewal price, and the cancellation rule in one line.
```

## How to use it

Put them on your site and your consult sheet.

From the Trainer Pack by 127. Use it in your own business and for your clients; please don't resell the pack.
