# Session packages

Builds three packages from your hourly rate, with the renewal price.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Build three personal training packages. My rate per session [$], sessions per week options [ ], package lengths [ ]. Give names, prices, what's included, a renewal price, and the cancellation rule in one line. Say the price, how often it charges and how to cancel, and make cancelling as easy as signing up.
```

## How to use it

Put them on your site and your consult sheet.

## Before you send

- Before anyone signs up, the plan says the price, how often it charges and how to cancel. Cancelling is as easy as signing up.
