# Mockup image prompts

Image prompts that put your design on a real-looking person in a place your buyer goes.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write five image prompts for mockups of a [product] with this design: [describe it]. The buyer is [niche]. Each prompt: the person, the place, the light, the camera angle and what they're doing. No celebrities, no brand logos, no real places' signage.
```

## How to use it

Use them in any image model. Label any AI image as a mockup on your page.
