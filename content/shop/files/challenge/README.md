# Run your own challenge

A five-day challenge is a free week where people build one real thing with you, a little each day. It's the format 127 uses for its own Day One Challenge. This course is that engine, with your days in it.

By the end you'll have the promise, the five days, the landing page, every email, the share mechanics, the run sheet, and the one honest offer at the end.

1. Pick the one thing people will have by Friday (01).
2. Design the five days so each one ends in something real (02).
3. Write the pages and the emails from the templates (03).
4. Add the share mechanics so the challenge spreads itself (04).
5. Plan the offer you make once, at the end (05).
6. Run it from the run sheet (06), inside the rules (07).

Each lesson ends with a prompt you can paste into any AI with your details filled in. Inside 127 HQ the matching workflows do the same job with your business facts already loaded.

Nothing in this course promises income, sales or results. What you get out of it depends on your business and the work you put in.

Use it in your own business and for your clients. Please don't resell or share the files themselves. Access for as long as we offer the course.
