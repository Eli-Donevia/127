# Make it spread itself

A challenge grows when finishing a day makes people want to show someone. Build the reason to share into each day instead of asking for shares.

## 1. A card every day

When someone finishes a day, give them an image sized for a story (1080 by 1920) with:

- The day and a four-word label: "DAY 1 · I'M LIVE"
- Their own result: their page address, their offer line, their count
- Your challenge name and handle, small

One tap saves it. Copy the caption at the same time so they don't have to write anything. Canva, Figma or a simple web page that draws the card all work.

## 2. Do it with a friend

On the joined page: "Doing this with someone? Send them your link." People finish more often with a partner, and every partner is a new join. If you can, and only when both of them said yes, show each friend's progress on the other's day pages ("Sam finished Day 2").

## 3. A real counter

"412 people are in" on the landing page works only if it's true and live. Never round up, never start from a fake number. Hide the counter until the number is one you're happy to show.

## 4. A wall, opt-in only

A page of finished work (first name, their thing, one line) is the best proof your challenge works. Ask with a separate, unticked box: "Show my [thing] on the wall". Hide the wall until it has enough entries that it doesn't look empty.

## 5. One hashtag, one handle

Put both in every caption template. If you reward sharing (a bonus, an entry, a free month), the caption says so, like "I get a bonus for sharing this." Reshare the cards of people who said yes to being featured.

## 6. Lives that make clips

If you go live, do the thing in front of people: build one participant's page from a comment, fix three offers on screen. A live that's a lecture makes no clips. A live where something gets built makes ten.

## Prompt: write the share kit

```text
My challenge: "Five days. [thing] live." Handle: [@handle]. Hashtag: [#tag].

Write, for each of the five days:
1. The card label (four words or fewer, capitals, starting "DAY [n] ·")
2. A one-line caption the participant posts with the card, in first person, that mentions the challenge and the hashtag naturally
3. One line for my own story when I reshare someone's card

If I reward sharing with [reward, or "nothing"], end each caption with a plain disclosure like "I get a bonus for sharing this."

Then write the friend invite line for the joined page, and the opt-in wording for a public wall of finished work.
```
