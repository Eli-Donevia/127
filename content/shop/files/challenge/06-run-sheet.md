# The run sheet

Three weeks before to one week after. Copy this into your calendar.

## Three weeks before

- Promise, five days and tools done (lessons 1 and 2).
- Landing page live with the join form. Test it on your own phone.
- Write your targets down now, before you see any numbers: joins, how many start Day 1, how many finish, how many buy.

## Two weeks before

- All nine emails written and scheduled (lesson 3). Send each one to yourself.
- Day page and share cards working (lesson 4). Finish Day 1 yourself, end to end.
- Film the five short day videos in one sitting.

## The week before

- Post about it every day. Show the thing people will have by Friday, not the challenge itself.
- Personally invite twenty people you know who fit. Ask each to bring one friend.
- Close registration the morning of Day 5 or keep it open; decide now and say it on the page.

## Challenge week

| Each morning | Each evening |
|---|---|
| Check the day email went out. | Reshare finished cards from people who said yes. |
| Post your own version of the day's move. | Reply to every question in one place (comments, a group, or email). |
| Watch where people stop on the day page. | Write tomorrow's stuck line if a new excuse showed up today. |

## The numbers that matter

- **Day 1 starts / joins.** Low means the promise excited people but the first step scared them. Make Day 1 smaller.
- **Day 5 finishes / Day 1 starts.** Low means one day is too big. Find the day with the steepest drop and halve it.
- **Cards shared / finishes.** Low means the card shows the challenge instead of their result. Put their thing first.
- **Buyers / finishers.** Low means the offer isn't the next step after what they built (lesson 5).

## The week after

- Send email 9.
- Ask finishers what almost made them quit. Their answers are next round's stuck lines.
- Write down what you'd cut. Then run it again. The second run is always better than the first.
