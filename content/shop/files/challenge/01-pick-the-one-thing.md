# Pick the one thing people will have by Friday

Most challenges fail on day one, before anyone joins: the promise is a feeling ("get unstuck", "find your why") instead of a thing. Feelings don't get shared. Things do.

Your promise is one sentence: **Five days. [One thing] live.** The thing has to pass three tests.

1. **It exists outside your challenge.** A page anyone can open, a post on their own account, a box in their fridge, a sent message. Not an answer typed into a form.
2. **A friend could see it.** If your participant can't show it to someone, they won't share it, and the challenge stops at the people you already reach.
3. **It's the first step of what you sell.** Your paid offer should be the obvious next step after the thing they built. If it isn't, you'll be pitching something unrelated on Friday and it will feel like a bait and switch.

## Examples by business

| Business | Weak promise | Strong promise |
|---|---|---|
| Photographer | Find your style | Five days. Your portfolio page live. |
| Meal prep | Eat better this week | Five days. Next week's lunches in your fridge. |
| Bookkeeper | Get your money right | Five days. One month of your books caught up. |
| Fitness coach | Build the habit | Five days. Your first week of training logged. |
| Web designer | Build your brand | Five days. Your homepage live. |
| Etsy seller | Grow your shop | Five days. Your next product listed. |

## Keep it small enough

Fifteen minutes a day is the bar for people with jobs. If your thing takes longer, cut it until it doesn't. Someone who finishes a small thing on Friday trusts you more than someone who quit a big thing on Wednesday.

## Prompt: pressure-test your promise

```text
I'm planning a free five-day challenge for [who it's for]. My paid offer is [what you sell, price].

My draft promise is: "Five days. [thing] live."

Check it against three tests and be blunt:
1. Does the thing exist outside the challenge, somewhere a friend could see it?
2. Can a beginner finish it in about 15 minutes a day for five days?
3. Is my paid offer the obvious next step after it?

Then give me five alternative promises that pass all three, each in the format "Five days. [thing] live." Rank them by how likely a participant is to show the result to a friend.
```
