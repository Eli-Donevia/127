# Review ask that follows the rules

A card and a short message asking regulars for a Google review, with no reward offered.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a small card for the counter and a two-line text for regulars asking for a Google review of [coffee shop]. Rules: no discount, freebie or reward for a review, ask everyone (not only happy customers), and include the link [link]. Warm and short.
```

## How to use it

Put the card by the register. Never trade a drink for a review.
