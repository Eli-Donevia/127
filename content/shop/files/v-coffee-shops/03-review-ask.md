# Review ask that follows the rules

A card and a short message asking regulars for a Google review, with no reward offered.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a small card for the counter and a two-line text for regulars asking for a Google review of [coffee shop]. Rules: no discount, freebie or reward for a review, ask everyone (not only happy customers), and include the link [link]. Warm and short. Each text names [business] and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
```

## How to use it

Put the card by the register. Never trade a drink for a review.

## Before you send

- Text only customers who gave you their number for texts (most booking apps ask at booking). Each one names your business and has STOP. Send between 8am and 8pm their time, one at a time or from your booking app, never to a list you bought or copied.
