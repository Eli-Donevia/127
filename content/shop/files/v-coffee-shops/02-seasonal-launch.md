# Seasonal drink launch

The post, the story frames and the counter sign for a new seasonal drink.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
We're launching [drink] on [date] at [coffee shop]. What's in it: [ ]. Price: [ ]. Write: the launch post caption, three story frames (tease, first pour, last call), and the counter sign (eight words at most plus the price).
```

## How to use it

Post the tease three days before. Print the sign.
