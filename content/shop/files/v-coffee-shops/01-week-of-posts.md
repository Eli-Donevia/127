# A week of posts from one shoot

Seven captions and a posting order from the photos you took in one 20-minute session.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
I shot these at [coffee shop] today: [list the photos or clips]. Write seven captions for this week, one a day, in this voice: [paste a caption you like]. Mix: one drink, one person behind the bar, one regular moment, one pastry, one hours or location post, one seasonal tease, one thank-you. Keep each under 40 words, no hashtag walls.
```

## How to use it

Shoot Monday morning before the rush. Schedule all seven at once.
