# Wholesale pitch to a local shop

A short, honest note to a gift shop or boutique asking if they'd carry your candles.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a short email to [shop name], a [kind of shop] in [town], asking if they'd carry candles from [my shop]. What I know about them: [something true you noticed]. My wholesale terms: [price per candle, minimum order]. Keep it under 120 words, one question at the end, no pressure. Add my name, business and mailing address at the bottom and a line that says if it's a no, I won't write again.
```

## How to use it

Send one at a time, to shops you'd actually want to be in.
