# Batch cost check

Works out what one candle costs you and what the price leaves for your time.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Help me price my candles. One batch makes [n] candles. Costs per batch: wax [$], fragrance oil [$], wicks [$], vessels [$], labels [$], packaging [$]. Pouring and curing take me [hours]. Show the cost per candle, what's left at [retail price] and at [wholesale price], and the price where my time is paid at [$ per hour]. Show the math in a table.
```

## How to use it

Run it every time a supplier price changes.
