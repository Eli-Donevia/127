# Neighbor flyer

A short flyer for the street you're working on.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a flyer for [business] to leave on the street where we just washed [house, only with the owner's OK]. 50 words at most: what we did, a price for the same job, how to book, and our name, phone and address. No discount pressure.
```

## How to use it

Leave them the same day. Ask the owner first.

From the Pressure Washing Pack by 127. Use it in your own business and for your clients; please don't resell the pack.
