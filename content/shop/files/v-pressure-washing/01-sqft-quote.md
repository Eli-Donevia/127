# Quote by square foot

Prices by surface and square foot, with a minimum and a soft wash line.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Build a pressure washing price sheet for [business]. Target per hour [$]. Rates by surface: concrete [$/sq ft], wood deck [ ], house soft wash by stories [ ], roof [ ], gutters [ ]. Minimum job [$]. Give a menu and a text I send with a quote from a customer's photos. Each text names [business] and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
```

## How to use it

Ask for photos and rough size before you drive out.

## Before you send

- Text only customers who gave you their number for texts (most booking apps ask at booking). Each one names your business and has STOP. Send between 8am and 8pm their time, one at a time or from your booking app, never to a list you bought or copied.
