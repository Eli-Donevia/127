# Frame product page

Writes one frame's product page from its measurements, material and the face shapes it suits.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
You write product pages for [brand], a small eyewear brand. Our voice: [three words]. Write the page for the [frame name] frame.

Facts (use only these):
- Material: [acetate, titanium...]
- Colors: [list]
- Lens width / bridge / temple: [mm]
- Face shapes it suits: [list]
- Price: [$]

Give me: a two-line opener that says who the frame is for, a fit section in plain words ("sits a little wide, good for a broad face"), one paragraph on how it is made, and three short answers to the questions people ask before buying frames online. No claims about vision, prescriptions or eye health.
```

## How to use it

Paste into Claude or ChatGPT once per frame. Keep the fit section; it answers the question that stops most online frame orders.
