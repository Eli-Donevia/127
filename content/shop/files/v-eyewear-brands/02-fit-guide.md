# Fit guide from your measurements

Turns your frame measurements into a fit guide a buyer can check against glasses they already own.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Here are the measurements for every frame we sell: [paste lens width, bridge, temple, frame width for each]. Write a one-page fit guide for our site.

1. Explain the three numbers printed inside a pair of glasses, in one sentence each.
2. Tell a buyer how to compare them with a pair they own.
3. Sort our frames into narrow, medium and wide, with the numbers that put them there.
4. End with what to do if they sit between two sizes.

Short sentences. No medical or prescription advice.
```

## How to use it

Put it on its own page and link it from every product page.
