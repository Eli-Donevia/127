## Before you use these

- If you sell lenses, check with your lens supplier: the FDA requires impact-tested lenses (21 CFR 801.410), and states set who may sell prescription lenses.
- No claims that blue light lenses protect eyes, ease strain or help sleep. UV claims ("UV400") only with your supplier's test report.
