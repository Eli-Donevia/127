# Try-on video brief

A 15-second try-on brief for one frame: the faces, the light, the first second and the shot order.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a 15-second try-on video brief for the [frame name] frame from [brand]. We film on a phone.

Include: who wears it on camera (face shape, style), where (window light, time of day), the first second (the frame going on, no logo card), five shots in order with what each one shows, the on-screen words (six words at most), and one line of caption. No music cues that need a license. No claims about vision.
```

## How to use it

Film three of these per new colorway: one for the product page, two for Reels and TikTok.
