Drag me up to full height, or flick me down to close. Esc works too.

How to use it

Mark up <dialog class="sht"> with a .sht-handle and a .sht-body, then call sheet(dialog, { snapPoints: [0.55, 1], scaleBackground: appEl }) and open(). Native dialog means focus stays inside and Esc closes it.

Using the attached file, open my booking form in a bottom sheet on phones: wrap the form in <dialog class="sht"> with a .sht-handle and .sht-body, add the sheet CSS and the module script, and open it from my "Book" button with snap points 0.55 and 1.
