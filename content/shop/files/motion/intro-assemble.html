How to use it

Good for trades, products, anything put together. Plays once in 1.2 to 2.4 seconds, then the page is fully usable. Phones get words instead of letters and no blur. Reduced motion and JavaScript off show the page complete and still.

  1. Copy the three <script> blocks at the end of this file to the end of your page's <body>, in the same order.
  2. Copy the <style> block's motion rules (everything after the first block of page styles) into your CSS.
  3. Mark your headline data-ci="head", your hero image or product data-ci="subject", and what follows (lead, buttons) data-ci="then".
  4. Optional: data-pop="line" on the headline draws one line in your color under the last word. Set your color with --pop.
