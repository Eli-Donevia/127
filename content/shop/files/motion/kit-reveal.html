How to use it

  1. Copy the one script at the end of this file to the end of your <body>, and the motion rules from the <style> block into your CSS.
  2. Add the attributes: data-rv (or ="mask", ="scale", ="left", ="right") to reveal, style="--d:2" to stagger, data-split for words that rise, data-par="0.12" for parallax, data-mag on a button, data-spot on a card, data-count on a number, and <div class="prog"> for a reading bar.
  3. It adds a Pause motion button to your footer. Keep it: it's what makes looping motion accessible.
