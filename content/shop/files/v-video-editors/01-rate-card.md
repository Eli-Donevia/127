# Rate card

Builds your rate card from your hours per deliverable and the hourly rate you want.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Help me build a rate card. I edit [types]. Time per deliverable: [e.g. short clip 45 min, 10-min YouTube 5 hours]. Target per hour: [$]. Rounds of revisions included: [ ]. Build three packages and per-piece prices, with a rush fee and what's not included. Then one paragraph I can paste into an email. Say the price, how often it charges and how to cancel, and make cancelling as easy as signing up.
```

## How to use it

Send it as a PDF or put it on your site's services section.

## Before you send

- Before anyone signs up, the plan says the price, how often it charges and how to cancel. Cancelling is as easy as signing up.
