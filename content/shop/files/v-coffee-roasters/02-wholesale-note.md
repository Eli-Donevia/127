# Wholesale note to a cafe

A short, specific note to one cafe owner about carrying your coffee.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a short email to [cafe] in [town] about carrying [roastery]'s coffee. What I noticed about them: [something true]. What I'd offer: [price per lb, minimum, delivery, a free tasting]. Under 120 words, one question. Sign with my name, business and mailing address, and say I won't follow up more than once.
```

## How to use it

Bring beans when they say yes to a tasting.
