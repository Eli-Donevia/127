# Wholesale note to a cafe

A short, specific note to one cafe owner about carrying your coffee.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a short email to [cafe] in [town] about carrying [roastery]'s coffee. What I noticed about them: [something true]. What I'd offer: [price per lb, minimum, delivery, a free tasting]. Under 120 words, one question. End with my name, my business, my mailing address [address] and "Reply 'no thanks' and I won't follow up."
```

## How to use it

Bring beans when they say yes to a tasting.

## Before you send

- Send each email yourself, one at a time, to a business you picked. Follow up once at most, and never after a no.
