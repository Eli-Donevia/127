# Tasting notes for the bag

Turns your cupping sheet into tasting notes and a bag page a non-expert understands.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Here's my cupping sheet for [coffee]: origin [ ], process [ ], roast level [ ], notes [ ], body [ ], acidity [ ]. Write: three tasting notes a regular coffee drinker would recognize, a two-sentence story of where it's from (only what I gave you), how to brew it (one method, one ratio), and a one-line label for the bag.
```

## How to use it

One per coffee. Put the brew line on the bag sticker too.
