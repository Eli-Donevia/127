# Roast day reel

A 15-second reel brief for roast day, with the sound moment planned.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a 15-second reel brief for roast day at [roastery]. Plan: the first second (beans dropping into the cooling tray), the first crack as the sound moment, four more shots in order, on-screen words (six at most), and a caption that names the coffee and when it ships.
```

## How to use it

Film it every roast day. Same shots, different coffee.
