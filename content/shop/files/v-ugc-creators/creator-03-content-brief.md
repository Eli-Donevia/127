# Content brief template

The one page that turns a brand's "can you make us a video?" into something you can film in one take. Send it to the brand to fill in, or fill it in yourself from the call and send it back for an OK before you film.

## The basics

- Brand and product: [ ]
- Where it will run: [TikTok, Instagram Reels, Meta ads, YouTube Shorts, the product page]
- Organic or paid use, and for how long: [ ]
- Length: [15, 30 or 60 seconds]
- Due date: [ ]

## Who it's for

- The buyer in one sentence: [for example "a busy mom who has tried three cleaners and hates all of them"]
- What they believe now: [ ]
- What we want them to believe after watching: [ ]

## The one message

[One sentence. If the video says only this, it worked.]

## Hooks to test (first 2 seconds)

1. [ ]
2. [ ]
3. [ ]

## Must show

- [the product in hand, the texture, the result, the packaging, the price]

## Must not say

- [claims the brand can't make: "cures", "guaranteed", medical words, competitor names]
