# Deal recap email

Recaps a brand deal in one email so nothing is left to memory.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a recap email to [brand contact] after we agreed: [deliverables], [deadline], [price], [usage rights and how long], [whitelisting yes/no], [raw footage yes/no], [payment terms]. Ask them to reply "confirmed". Friendly and short.
```

## How to use it

Send it the same day you agree, before you film.
