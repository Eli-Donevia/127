# Creator brief

A UGC brief for one product: the shots, what to say and the words to stay away from.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a UGC brief for [product] from [brand]. Include: who the creator is talking to, the first two seconds, five shots, three things they can say in their own words (texture, scent, how they use it), the words they must not use (cures, fixes, treats, any skin condition by name, SPF or sun protection, antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, collagen-boosting, detox, chemical-free), that they use the product before filming and say only what they found, no before-and-after, the #ad line, and the usage rights we're asking for: [where and how long].
```

## How to use it

Send it with the product. Keep the do-not-say list in every brief.
