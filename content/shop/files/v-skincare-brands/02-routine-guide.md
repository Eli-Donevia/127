# Routine guide

Builds a morning and night routine from the products you sell, in the order to use them.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Here are the products [brand] sells: [list with what each is]. Build a simple morning routine and a night routine from them, in order, with one line on how much to use and how. Add a short version for someone who owns one product. No promises about results.
```

## How to use it

Use it as a page, a carousel and the second welcome email.
