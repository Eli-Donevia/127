## Before you use these

- This pack writes the words. It doesn't make a product or label legal. FDA's cosmetics law (MoCRA) applies to small brands too: a US contact on the label for reporting problems, safety records, and reporting serious reactions.
- Never say SPF or sun protection, name a condition (like acne, eczema or rosacea), or say antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, collagen-boosting or detox. Keep proof on file before you say clinically proven, dermatologist tested or recommended, hypoallergenic, non-comedogenic, non-toxic, clean, natural or organic. Never "chemical-free".
- Creators use the product before filming and say only what they found. No before-and-after.
