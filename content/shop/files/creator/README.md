# 127 Creator Kit

Everything to start selling UGC to brands: the portfolio site, the rate card, the pitch emails, the brief and the usage rights checklist, plus the creator-side templates from the Store.

1. Build your portfolio with 05-portfolio-site.md and the Creator website file.
2. Fill in your rate card (01) from your own costs. The Pricing Lab sheet helps.
3. Pitch one brand a day with the emails in 02.
4. Send the brief (03) after every yes, and settle usage (04) before you film.

Use them in your own business and for your clients. Please don't resell or share the files themselves.
