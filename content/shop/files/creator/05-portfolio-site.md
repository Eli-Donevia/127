# Your portfolio site, built tonight

What goes on your portfolio page, in order, and the prompt that builds it from the Creator website design in this kit (or any site builder). Brands decide in about one scroll, so order matters.

## What goes on it, top to bottom

1. **Who you make content for**, in one line. Not "content creator": "Short video for pet brands, filmed with my two dogs."
2. **Three to six videos**, playing on mute, best one first. Real brand work if you have it. If you don't, film spec ads for products you already use and label them "Spec ad, not paid".
3. **Brands you've worked with**, only real ones.
4. **What you offer**, from your rate card: the main packages, with or without prices.
5. **How it works**, in three steps: brief, film, deliver.
6. **One way to reach you**: an email or a short form. Not five links.

## The prompt

Paste this into Claude, Lovable or Bolt with the Creator website file attached:

```text
Build my UGC portfolio from the attached site design. Keep its layout and motion.
Me: [name], I make [type of video] for [type of brand], based in [city].
Videos (in this order): [links].
Brands I've worked with: [names, or leave the section out].
Packages: [paste from my rate card].
Contact: [email].
Replace every sample word and photo with mine. Label any spec ad "Spec ad, not paid".
Keep the privacy notice link in the footer.
```

## Before you share it

- [ ] Opens fast on a phone
- [ ] Every video has a poster image, so the page isn't blank while it loads
- [ ] Your email works (send yourself a test)
- [ ] Spec work is labeled
