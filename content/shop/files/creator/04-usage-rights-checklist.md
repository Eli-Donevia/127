# Usage rights and whitelisting checklist

What to settle in writing before you film, so a brand doesn't run your face in ads for a year on a one-time fee. This is a checklist of the questions to answer, not legal advice. For a large deal or anything unusual, have a lawyer read the contract.

## Before you agree

- [ ] Who can post it: only the brand's own accounts, or their partners and retailers too?
- [ ] Organic only, or paid ads as well?
- [ ] For how long? Write an end date, not "ongoing".
- [ ] Which places: social, website, email, TV, print, in-store?
- [ ] Can they edit it, cut it shorter, or add text and music?
- [ ] Whitelisting or Spark Ads (ads run from your account): how long, and can you see the spend?
- [ ] Exclusivity: are you agreeing not to work with competitors? For how long, and is it paid?
- [ ] Can you show the work in your portfolio?

## Put in the agreement

- [ ] Deliverables, length and number of edits
- [ ] The fee, the deposit and when the rest is due
- [ ] Usage: where, organic or paid, and the end date
- [ ] What happens after the end date (they stop running it, or pay to extend)
- [ ] Who owns the raw footage
- [ ] Disclosure: paid posts are labeled #ad or with the platform's paid partnership tool, every time
- [ ] Say only what's true for you: use the product before you film it, no filters or edits on a before-and-after, and no health or results claims the brand can't back up.

## After it's live

- [ ] Put the end date in your calendar
- [ ] Two weeks before it, send the renewal quote
- [ ] If an ad is still running after the end date, email the brand with the date and your renewal price
