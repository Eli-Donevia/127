# Deposit and late policy

A clear deposit, late and no-show policy, and the text you send after a no-show.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a booking policy for [business]: deposit [$, refundable or not, when], late arrival (grace period [min], what happens after), no-shows, cancellations ([hours] notice), and fills older than [weeks]. Friendly and firm. Then the text I send after a no-show. Each text names [business] and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
```

## How to use it

Put it on your booking page and in the confirmation.

## Before you send

- Text only customers who gave you their number for texts (most booking apps ask at booking). Each one names your business and has STOP. Send between 8am and 8pm their time, one at a time or from your booking app, never to a list you bought or copied.
