# Deposit and late policy

A clear deposit, late and no-show policy, and the text you send after a no-show.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write a booking policy for [business]: deposit [$, refundable or not, when], late arrival (grace period [min], what happens after), no-shows, cancellations ([hours] notice), and fills older than [weeks]. Friendly and firm. Then the text I send after a no-show.
```

## How to use it

Put it on your booking page and in the confirmation.
