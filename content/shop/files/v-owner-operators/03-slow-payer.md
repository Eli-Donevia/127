# Slow payer follow-up

A polite, firm series of emails for an invoice past its terms.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write three short emails to [broker] about invoice [number] for load [number], delivered [date], terms [days]. Email 1 on the due date (friendly check), email 2 at 7 days late (firm, ask for a pay date), email 3 at 15 days late (final, mention next steps I name: [ ]). No threats, no legal language.
```

## How to use it

Send from the email on your invoice. Keep the thread together.
