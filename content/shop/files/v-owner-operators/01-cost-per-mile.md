# Cost per mile

Works out your real cost per mile from your monthly costs and miles, and the lowest rate worth taking.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Help me find my cost per mile. Monthly: truck payment [$], insurance [$], permits and plates [$], phone and ELD [$], parking [$]. Per mile: fuel [$ per gallon] at [mpg], maintenance set-aside [$], tires [$]. I run about [miles] a month and want to pay myself [$] a month. Show fixed cost per mile, variable cost per mile, total, and the lowest rate per loaded mile I should take with [%] deadhead. Table, then two sentences.
```

## How to use it

Run it monthly and whenever fuel moves. Keep the number on your phone's lock screen.
