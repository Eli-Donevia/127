# Batch day plan

Plans five videos to film in one afternoon from your topics.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Plan a batch day for my [platform] channel about [topic]. I have [hours] and [locations]. Give five videos: the hook, three beats, the shot list, what to wear (one change at most) and the order to film them so I move as little as possible.
```

## How to use it

Film on Sunday, schedule all five.
