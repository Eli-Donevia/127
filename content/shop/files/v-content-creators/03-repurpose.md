# One video, five pieces

Cuts one long video into five short clips and a carousel, with timestamps.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Here's the transcript of my video with timestamps: [paste]. Find five 20 to 40-second clips that stand alone (give start and end times and a hook line for each) and turn the main point into a six-slide carousel.
```

## How to use it

Run it on every long video you post.
