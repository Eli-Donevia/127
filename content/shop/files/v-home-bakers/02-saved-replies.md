# Saved replies for order DMs

Answers to the six questions home bakers get every day, in your voice.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Write saved replies for [bakery] for: price list, allergens (we bake with [ ]; we can't promise allergen-free), pickup times and place, custom cake requests (what I need to quote), delivery (yes/no and cost), and "are you sold out?". Each under 50 words, friendly, ending with the order link [link].
```

## How to use it

Save them in Instagram's saved replies or your phone's text shortcuts.
