# Holiday preorder plan

A preorder plan for one holiday: the menu, the cutoff, the posts and the pickup slots.

## The prompt

Copy everything in the box into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets] first.

```
Plan [holiday] preorders for [bakery]. I can bake [max] orders. Menu ideas: [ ]. Give: a 4-item holiday menu with prices, the cutoff date, pickup slots, three posts (announce, half sold, last call) and a pickup reminder text.
```

## How to use it

Start three weeks out. Close orders at your max, not after.
