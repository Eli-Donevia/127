## Before you use these

- Before you take orders online, check your state's cottage food rules: whether you need a permit, the home-kitchen notice on labels (some states want it online too), which foods are allowed (many leave out cakes that need the fridge), delivery and shipping, and any sales cap. Most states don't allow cottage food across state lines.
