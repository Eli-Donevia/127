# 127 Founder OS

*Your business in one place: roadmap, pipeline, conversations, content, numbers and weekly review. Built for the 127 roadmap.*

## Start here

1. Open **30-Day Roadmap**. Filter Track to yours (Start, Build or Grow). Do this week's steps in order.
2. Put 20 names in **Pipeline** today. Every open row gets a next step and a date.
3. Log every discovery call in **Conversations**, in their words.
4. Plan week one in **Content Calendar**. Weeks two to four get topics only.
5. Every Monday, add a row to **Weekly Scorecard**, then open **Weekly CEO Review**.

## What's inside

- **30-Day Roadmap**: all four 127 tracks, every step tied to its template and playbook.
- **Pipeline**: Every person you might sell to, with a stage and a next step date.
- **Conversations**: Your discovery conversations: who, when, what they said.
- **Content Calendar**: 30 days of posts with pillar, hook, format and results.
- **Hook Bank**: Fill-in-the-blank hooks by type, each with a filled example.
- **Weekly Scorecard**: Your five numbers every Monday, plus your call for the week.
- **Objection Log**: Every "but" you hear, in their words, and how you answered it.
- **One-Page Offer**: the 127 template as a page you fill in.
- **Weekly CEO Review**: the 127 template as a page you fill in.
- **One-Page SOP**: the 127 template as a page you fill in.
- **30-Day Retro**: the 127 template as a page you fill in.
- **Customer Interview Kit**: the 127 template as a page you fill in.

## After you import

- Check property types. Notion imports some columns as text. Set Stage, Status, Type, Pillar and Format to Select, and dates to Date.
- Delete the example rows when you have your own. They show how a filled-in row looks.
- Want math, like conversion rates and follow-up dates? Use the spreadsheet versions in the Library. Notion is for tracking, the sheets are for numbers.
