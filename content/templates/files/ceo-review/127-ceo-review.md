# Weekly CEO Review

*A 30-minute weekly review in six time-boxed parts that turns last week's numbers into one cut, one handoff and next week's top three.*

**Template** · Grow track · Builder · 30 minutes

**You walk away with:** Last week's numbers, one thing cut, one task on its way to a person or tool, and next week's top three on your calendar.

**Use it:** Grow track week 4, when you cut the activity that produced nothing, then every week at the same time.

**Pairs with:** Weekly CEO Review (playbook)

---

Thirty minutes. Six parts. Five minutes each. The point is a decision, not a journal entry.

Book a recurring slot before you fill this in. Sunday evening or Monday morning works for most people. Phone on do not disturb. This is the half hour a week you act like the CEO instead of the only employee.

## 2. What worked and why (5 minutes)

Don't stop at 'that post did well'. Ask what it did that the others didn't.

**What moved a number**
[One thing: the post, message, offer or person]

**Why it worked better than before**
[The hook? The offer? Who you messaged? When you posted?]

**How I'll do more of it next week**
[Same format, same channel, same message]

> *“Do more of what works, not just more.”*
>
> Eli

## 3. What to cut (5 minutes)

Something comes off your list every week. Trying to do too much was one of Eli's biggest mistakes.

**Took time, produced nothing**
[Be honest. Count the hours.]

**Kill or pause?**
[Kill: fair try, nothing moved. Pause: fewer than three clean tries, so run it once more.]

## 4. Delegate or automate one thing (5 minutes)

Find one task you repeated that didn't need your judgment. Write it down even if you can't hand it off yet. The list becomes your plan.

**Task I repeated**
[Same steps every time]

**Who or what could do it**
[Saved reply, automation, AI tool or a person]

**First step to hand it off**
[e.g. record it once for a One-Page SOP]

| If you keep doing this | Hand it to |
| --- | --- |
| Answering the same DMs | Saved Replies, or ManyChat for keyword replies |
| Typing up call notes | Granola, then a follow-up template |
| Writing captions and first drafts | Claude, with your Hook Bank as input |
| Researching competitors or a market | Perplexity |
| Texting back and forth to book times | A Calendly booking link |
| A bigger task you repeat | A One-Page SOP, then a person or tool |

- [ ] Make number 1 the one that moves revenue or customers most.
- [ ] Put all three on your calendar before you close this page.

> A priority without a time slot is a wish. Can't fit all three? Cut one, or hand off the task from part 4.

## 6. Perspective check (5 minutes)

Step back from the business. Building in vain, as Eli puts it, means chasing money while neglecting your faith and the people who matter. Answer honestly. Nobody grades this.

**Did I keep my commitments to the people closest to me this week?**
[your answer]

**Did I make time for my faith, rest and health, or did the business eat all of it?**
[your answer]

**Did I do business with integrity: no shortcuts, no promises I couldn't keep?**
[your answer]

**Who helped me this week, and have I thanked them?**
[your answer]

**Am I still building for the reason I started?**
[your answer]

> *“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labor in vain.”*
>
> Psalm 127:1 (NIV)

>
> A short-form video editing service for podcasters (hypothetical).
>
> 1. Numbers: Reach 9,400. Visits 180. Leads 6, up from 2. Sales 2, same as last week. Revenue $300.
> Biggest change: leads tripled. Biggest drop in a rate: leads to sales. 4 new leads haven't bought yet.
>
> 2. What worked: a before-and-after clip of a client's podcast brought in 4 of the 6 DMs.
> Why: it showed the result in the first second. My other posts opened with me talking.
> More of it: three more before-and-after clips next week, same format.
>
> 3. Cut: two hours redesigning the logo. Zero impact. Kill. The logo stays as is.
>
> 4. Delegate: answering 'what's your turnaround?' DMs. A saved reply this week, a One-Page SOP next week.
>
> 5. Top three:
> 1. Post three before-and-after clips. Moves leads. Mon, Wed, Fri at 7 am.
> 2. Follow up with all 4 open leads. Moves sales. Tuesday at 6 pm.
> 3. Ask both new clients for one referral each. Moves leads. Thursday at 6 pm.
>
> 6. Perspective: missed Sunday dinner with family to edit, so editing moves to Saturday morning. Told one client their edit would be a day late instead of rushing it. Thanked my roommate for filming help.
