# Batch Day

*Film a full week of short videos in one two-hour block: plan, film, edit and schedule five posts, with a shot list that keeps you on track.*

**Checklist** · Grow track · Free · 120 minutes

**You walk away with:** Five edited, captioned posts scheduled for the week, a filled shot list and a list of questions to answer in next week's batch.

**Use it:** Grow track week 2, once your three content pillars are set, then once a week in the same slot.

**Pairs with:** 30-Day Content Calendar (playbook)

---

Posting every day doesn't mean filming every day. Two focused hours beat seven scattered days.

Block one slot, make everything at once and schedule it. The times below add up to two hours. CapCut is free and good enough to start.

### Before you start

- [ ] Put your two-hour slot on the calendar, same time every week.
- [ ] Charge your phone and clear space for video.
- [ ] Clean the camera lens.
- [ ] Pick one spot with window light facing you.
- [ ] Pull last week's comments and DMs for post ideas.
- [ ] Open your three pillars and your Hook Bank, or a note of hooks you've saved.

### Plan (15 minutes)

- [ ] Write five hooks, with at least one from each pillar.
- [ ] Fill each hook's blanks with your customers' exact words.
- [ ] Write a one-line outline under each hook.
- [ ] Pick one format per post.
- [ ] Fill in the shot list below.

Formats to try. Change one thing at a time so you know what made the difference.

- Talking head with a text hook
- Green screen over a screenshot
- Voiceover on a before and after
- A list of three
- A day in the life

### Film (45 minutes)

- [ ] Set the phone at eye level and lock focus.
- [ ] Record a 10-second test and check the sound.
- [ ] Film all five back to back in the same spot.
- [ ] Start talking on the first frame. No warm-up.
- [ ] Shoot each hook two or three ways.
- [ ] Grab extra clips: hands at work, before and after, your setup.

### Edit in CapCut (45 minutes)

- [ ] Pick the best take of each hook.
- [ ] Cut every pause and every 'um'.
- [ ] Add auto captions and fix any wrong words.
- [ ] Put the hook on screen in the first two seconds.
- [ ] Keep each video under 45 seconds.
- [ ] Watch each one on your phone with the sound off.

### Captions and schedule (15 minutes)

- [ ] Write a short caption for each post.
- [ ] Give each post one call to action: follow, comment or DM.
- [ ] Schedule all five with the platform's own scheduler.
- [ ] Check the days match your posting rhythm.

### After

- [ ] Log each post in your 30-Day Content Planner.
- [ ] Reply to every comment within a day.
- [ ] Write down every question people ask. That's next week's batch.
- [ ] Fill in the format tracker 48 hours after each post.
