# How to set up the Client Portal in Notion

One page per client: welcome, what's included, timeline, files, shared tasks and how to reach you.

## Import it (about 5 minutes, on a computer)

Notion import is not in the phone app.

1. Download **127-notion-client-portal.zip**. Do not unzip it.
2. In Notion, open **Settings**, then **Import**. (You can also click **Import** near the bottom of the sidebar.)
3. Choose **Text & Markdown** and pick the ZIP. Markdown files become pages and CSV files become databases inside them.
4. Wait for the import to finish, then open **Client Portal** in your sidebar. Drag it to where you want it in your workspace.

If the ZIP import puts the databases somewhere odd, import each CSV on its own: **Settings > Import > CSV**. Each one becomes its own database. Then drag it onto the home page.

## Set up first (10 minutes)

1. Open **Client Portal** and fill in everything that is the same for every client: what's included, how you work, your reply times. This is your master copy.
2. Open **Shared Tasks** and change these property types (click the column name, then **Edit property > Type**):
   - Owner: **Select** (Client, and your business name).
   - Status: **Status** or **Select** (To do, In progress, Waiting, Done).
   - Due: **Date**. Step: **Select**.
3. Delete the "For you" note at the top of your master copy once you've read it.

## For each new client

1. In the sidebar, hover over **Client Portal**, click **...** and choose **Duplicate**.
2. Rename the copy with the client's name and fill in the brackets: name, dates, links.
3. Click **Share** at the top right. Invite the client by email, or turn on **Publish** for a read-only link. Give **Can edit** only if they should tick tasks off.
4. Check the copy's Shared Tasks: it belongs to this client only.

Tip: keep your master copy private, so a client never sees another client's page.

*Notion menu names change. These steps were checked in October 2026.*
