> **Filled example: a print-on-demand brand**
>
> A print-on-demand shirt brand (hypothetical).
>
> Where I am now, last 30 days: Reach 18,000. Visits 420. Leads 25. Sales 9. Revenue $270. 6 hours a week.
> Biggest bottleneck: visits to sales. People look, few buy.
>
> One outcome: 60 orders in 90 days.
> Why now: holiday gifts are when people buy shirts.
>
> Priority 1: fix the store page.
> Why: the leak is between visits and orders. Measure: visits to orders, from 2% to 4%. First step: run the Landing Page Leak Audit on Sunday. Owner: me.
>
> Priority 2: post four times a week on TikTok.
> Why: reach feeds everything else. Measure: 4 posts a week for 12 weeks. First step: batch day on Saturday at 10 am. Owner: me, with CapCut for edits.
>
> Priority 3: launch a three-shirt gift bundle.
> Why: more revenue per order. Measure: bundles sold. First step: ask 10 past buyers which designs to include. Owner: me, with Shopify for the listing.
>
> Stop list: no second platform, no new product line, no paid ads until the page converts, no logo redesign.
> Milestones: month 1, page fixed and 15 orders. Month 2, bundle live and 35 orders total. Month 3, 60 orders.
> Weekly rhythm: CEO review Sunday at 7 pm. Batch day Saturday at 10 am. Outreach Tuesday and Thursday at 8 pm, 30 minutes.
> Risk: finals in month 2. Plan: batch two weeks of posts before finals start.
> People: mentor, a local print shop owner I'll ask for coffee. Peer, a friend running a candle brand, check-in every other Friday.
> Perspective: protecting Sunday mornings for church and family. A win even if I miss 60: I learned what makes people buy.
