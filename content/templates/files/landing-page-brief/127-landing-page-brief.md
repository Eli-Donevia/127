## The seven blocks, in order

Visitors scroll top to bottom on a phone. Each block answers the next question in their head.

**1. Headline**
[The result they want, in their words. Not your business name. Not a slogan.]

**2. Subhead**
[For [who] who want [result], [how it works]. One sentence.]

**3. Three benefits**
[Outcomes, not features. "Your sneakers look new in 48 hours" beats "premium cleaning solution".]

**4. Proof**
[Real only: conversations, pilot results, photos, your story. See the list below.]

## Honest proof when you have no customers

Fake proof kills trust the second someone spots it. You don't need testimonials to launch. You need something true that lowers the risk of saying yes.

> *“Win their trust, win their wallet.”*
>
> Eli

- Your research: "Built after talking to 15 lawn care customers in our neighborhood."
- Your own experience: "I cleaned my own collection for three years before offering it."
- A free pilot: do it for two or three people at no cost, for honest feedback and permission to share results.
- Photos and screenshots of real work, with permission.
- A guarantee. Taking the risk off them shows you believe in it.
- Nothing true to show yet? Leave the section out. Never fill it with something made up.

## Let Claude write the first draft

Fill the brackets from your brief and paste this into Claude. Then edit the draft into your own voice before it goes live.

**Prompt for Claude** (copy and fill the [brackets])

```
You're helping me write a one-page landing page for my first business. Most visitors will read it on a phone.

Business: [what you sell, in one sentence].
Customer: [who it's for].
Their exact words for the problem: [2 or 3 phrases from customer conversations].
Headline draft: [headline].
Three benefits: [benefit 1], [benefit 2], [benefit 3].
Proof I actually have: [real proof only].
Offer: [what they get], [price], [guarantee].
Top objections: [objection 1], [objection 2], [objection 3].
One call to action: [button text], goes to [checkout / booking link / form].
Building in: [Shopify / Carrd / Framer].

Write the copy block by block, in this order: headline, subhead, three benefits, proof, offer, FAQ, call to action.

Rules:
- Short, plain sentences. Write for a phone screen.
- Use my customers' words wherever you can.
- Benefits are outcomes, not features.
- Frame the cost as the smallest number and the value as the largest.
- Don't invent testimonials, numbers, reviews, logos or urgency.
- One call to action, repeated at the top and bottom.

Then give me three other headlines in my customers' words, and tell me which block is weakest and why.
```

Want Claude to build the page too? Once the copy is right, ask: "Turn this into a single-page HTML site."

## Five-point pre-launch check

Run it on your phone, not your laptop, before you share the link with anyone.

> *“How you do one thing is how people perceive you do most things.”*
>
> Eli

- [ ] Five-second test: someone new sees it for five seconds and can say what you sell and who it's for.
- [ ] Phone check: every block reads cleanly, nothing is cut off and the button is easy to tap.
- [ ] Click test: every button and link works. I made a real purchase or booking, then refunded or canceled it.
- [ ] Honesty check: every claim is true, and I can back it up.
- [ ] Follow-up check: after someone clicks, they get a confirmation and I get notified.

> Failed the five-second test? Rewrite the headline first.

> **Filled example: resume review**
>
> Page goal: book a review.
> Tool: Carrd, with a booking link.
>
> 1. Headline: Get more interviews from the same job applications.
> 2. Subhead: A line-by-line resume rewrite for college seniors applying to internships, back in 48 hours.
> 3. Benefits: Recruiters see your strongest work first. Every bullet shows a result, not a duty. You stop guessing what's wrong.
> 4. Proof: Built from 12 conversations with college seniors and two campus recruiters. First five reviews free, results shared with permission.
> 5. Offer: Rewrite, a tailored version and a video walkthrough. $49 one time, less than $1 a week for a year. Don't love it? Free redo, then a full refund.
> 6. FAQ:
> Is this AI? I write every rewrite myself. AI only checks spelling.
> Will it work for my major? It's built for business and finance internships. Other majors, DM me first.
> What if I hate it? I redo it free. Still hate it? Full refund.
> 7. Call to action: "Book my review" at the top and bottom. Goes to my booking link.
