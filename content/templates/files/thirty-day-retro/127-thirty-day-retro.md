> **Filled example: a lawn care side business**
>
> A lawn care side business on the Start track (hypothetical).
>
> Focus: prove people will pay. Goal: 3 paying lawn clients.
> Hit it? Partly. 2 paying clients. I started outreach in week 3 instead of week 1.
>
> Numbers, day 1 to day 30:
> Reach: 0 to 1,900 (flyers and neighborhood group posts).
> Visits: 0 to 60 (booking page).
> Leads: 0 to 9. Sales: 0 to 2. Revenue: $0 to $180.
> Conversations had: 0 to 14. Hours per week: 3 to 6.
>
> Wins: first interview, first flyer run, first paid mow, first referral.
> What worked: knocking on doors with a flyer right after mowing a neighbor's lawn.
> Why: people saw the finished lawn next door. The proof was right there.
> What didn't: posting in a city-wide group. 2 replies, 0 sales.
> What got in the way: waiting until the flyer looked perfect.
> Biggest lesson: talk to people where they can see the work.
>
> Keep: door knocking after every job. Cut: city-wide group posts. Test: a referral card for every client.
> Thank: my first client, who sent his neighbor. My sister, who designed the flyer.
>
> Stage changed? Yes, first sale. Retaking the assessment.
> Top three: 1) 5 paying clients, moves sales. 2) Knock on 40 doors, moves leads. 3) A referral card for every client, moves leads.
> First step tomorrow: print 50 flyers.
> Perspective: still building to pay for school without debt. Missed two family dinners, so jobs move to Saturday mornings.
