# Landing Page Leak Audit

*Run 24 quick checks on a page people visit but don't buy from, find the three biggest leaks and fix them one at a time.*

**Checklist** · Grow track · Builder · 45 minutes

**You walk away with:** Your page checked on a phone, your top three leaks with a fix for each, and a before-and-after number that shows whether each fix worked.

**Use it:** Grow track week 3, or any week your Weekly Founder Scorecard shows visits going up while leads stay flat.

**Pairs with:** Landing Page Brief (playbook), Founder KPI Dashboard (playbook)

---

People visit. Nobody buys. That's a leak, and more traffic won't fix it. More people on a leaky page just means more people leaving.

Run every check on your phone, not your laptop. Tick what passes. Anything you can't tick is a leak. Then pick your top three and fix one at a time.

### Clarity

- [ ] Show the page to a stranger for five seconds, then ask what you sell and who it's for.
- [ ] Make the headline the result customers want, in their words.
- [ ] Make the subhead say who it's for and how it works.

### Mobile

- [ ] Open the page on your phone.
- [ ] Check the button shows without scrolling.
- [ ] Check every block reads cleanly, with nothing cut off.
- [ ] Load it on mobile data. If you're waiting, shrink or cut images.

### Offer

- [ ] List exactly what they get.
- [ ] Frame the price as the smallest true number, like per week or per session.
- [ ] Offer only a guarantee you can honor every time.
- [ ] Promise only a delivery time you've already tested.

### Proof

- [ ] Show only real proof: conversations, photos of real work, pilot results, your story.
- [ ] Delete any number, review or logo you can't back up.
- [ ] Get permission for every customer quote and photo.

> *“Trust beats everything else in sales.”*
>
> Eli

### Objections

- [ ] Pull your top three objections from your Objection Log or your DMs.
- [ ] Answer all three plainly on the page.

### Action

- [ ] Use one call to action: one button, one next step.
- [ ] Repeat that same button at the top and the bottom.
- [ ] Tap every button and link on your phone.
- [ ] Complete a real purchase or booking, then refund or cancel it.

### Follow-up

- [ ] Check the buyer gets a confirmation message right away.
- [ ] Check you get notified of every sale, booking or form.

### Tracking

- [ ] Write down this week's visits and leads from your Weekly Founder Scorecard.
- [ ] Work out visits to leads (leads divided by visits). That's your before number.

> *“How you do one thing is how people perceive you do most things.”*
>
> Eli
