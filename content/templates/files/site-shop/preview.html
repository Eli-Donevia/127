Maya, Ember & Oak

Made by hand, in small batches.

I started pouring candles in my kitchen in 2021 because I couldn't find one that smelled like a real campfire without the headache. Every candle is poured by hand in small batches of 24, with soy wax, cotton wicks and phthalate-free oils.
