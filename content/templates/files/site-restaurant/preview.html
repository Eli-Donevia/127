Our story

Tortillas pressed by hand, everything touched by the fire

Casa Lumbre started as a Sunday cookout in Marisol's backyard. Everything still runs through the wood fire: the tortillas, the meat, even the salsa. We make the masa every morning and keep the menu short so every plate leaves the kitchen right.

Marisol
