# Brand Sheet

*Set up a name, positioning line, voice, two colors, one font, a wordmark and matching handles in one afternoon, on one page you can send anyone.*

**Template** · Build track · Builder · 120 minutes

**You walk away with:** A one-page brand sheet with your name, positioning line, three voice words, two colors, one font, a wordmark and one handle used everywhere.

**Use it:** Build track week 2, in one afternoon before you rebuild your landing page, then leave it alone for three months.

**Pairs with:** Brand Basics in an Afternoon (playbook)

---

Your brand is not your logo. It's what people expect from you, based on every interaction they've had with you.

A first brand doesn't need to be impressive. It needs to be clear and consistent. Give it one afternoon. Still picking colors at hour four? Stop and choose.

## Step 3: Three voice words

Voice is how you sound in captions, emails and DMs. Pick three words and give each a "this, not that".

| Word | Means this | Not that |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct | "Here's what's wrong with your resume." | "Your resume is bad." |

|   |   |   |

Read your last five captions or DMs out loud. If they don't sound like your three words, rewrite one now.

### Make a wordmark logo

1. Type your name in your font and main color. That's a real logo. Many big brands use one.
2. Build it in Canva or another free design tool. Want a symbol? Ask an AI image tool for a few simple options.
3. Shrink it to profile-photo size. Can't read it? Simplify.
4. Export a square version for profiles and a wide version for your website header.
5. Save both in one folder and put the link on your brand sheet.

## Your brand sheet

Put it all on one page. Save it in Notion or your notes app. Send it to anyone who makes something for you, from a friend designing a flyer to a freelancer editing a video.

**Brand sheet** (copy and fill the [brackets])

```
BRAND SHEET: [Business name]

Positioning: I help [customer] get [result] without [pain].

Voice: [word 1], [word 2], [word 3].
[Word 1] means [this], not [that].
[Word 2] means [this], not [that].
[Word 3] means [this], not [that].

Main color: [name] [code]
Accent color: [name] [code]
Font: [font name]
Logo files: [link to both versions]

Handle: @[handle] on [platforms]
Bio: [your bio]
One link: [URL]
```
