Questions coaches ask

Do I need a website already?

No. The kit builds one.

Can my clients use the app on their phone?

Yes. They open a link and add it to their home screen. It works offline and saves every set.

Will it write fake testimonials or results?

No. The kit leaves a spot for a client's own words, used only with their written OK, and never writes weight, body or money claims.

Is it only for fitness coaches?

It's built for fitness first. Nutrition, run and mindset coaches can use it today; their own templates come next.
