# Meal prep and personal chef pack

Start meal prep and personal chef work on the 127 spine. The member cooks in the client's own kitchen with groceries bought for them. Selling meals cooked in your own home kitchen falls under state cottage food and health rules, and the pack says so with a source wherever it comes up. Food safety first: every allergy in writing on the paper intake before the first cook day, recipes that leave those foods out, and a label on every container with its eat-by date, reheat steps and what the dish contains.

Tools: `mealprep.list_first25` (code, 2), `mealprep.build_offer` (1), `mealprep.answer_reply` (ai write, 2), `mealprep.plan_quote` (1), `mealprep.log_client` (1), `mealprep.make_intake_form` (1), `mealprep.plan_week_menu` (1, the signature), `mealprep.ask_reviews` (1), `mealprep.draft_story` (1), `mealprep.make_story_card` (2, render), `mealprep.list_next25` (2), `mealprep.ready_facts` (1). Composes `sales.quote`, `sales.invoice`, `site.build`, `local.gbp_kit`, `local.flyer`.

- Data: `data/niche.json` (sources in `research/niches/mealprep.json`, checked 2026-10-19), `data/storage.json` (fridge and freezer temperatures, the 2 hour rule, safe cooking and reheating temperatures, storage times by kind of dish and the 9 major allergens, each with its FDA, CDC or Florida source) and `data/recipes.json` (28 home dinners with ingredients by store section and allergen tags, cook times, the safe temperature each one cooks to, how it reheats and whether it freezes).
- The week menu is pure code (`lib/menu.js`): no AI, no network. It leaves out every recipe that needs a food on the "Foods to leave out (allergies included)" list, keeps the way they eat and the foods they avoid, rotates last week's dinners out, merges and scales the shopping list, and labels storage from `data/storage.json`. Dinners past the fridge window go to the freezer with a thaw night and a best-quality date. It never calls a dish free of anything: the plan says "Left out: peanuts, shellfish", packaged items say which foods to read the label for, a dish with none of the 9 major allergens says "None of the 9 major allergens in the recipe. Packaged ingredients can differ.", and the cook day has a step to check each package before labeling.
- A diet the client names is a preference to cook to, never a prescription, and the list stays as it is (no Halal, Kosher or medical diets). There is no medical-word check: every plan says, word for word, "This menu cooks to the preferences typed here. It isn't a medical diet. If a doctor or dietitian gave anyone a meal plan, cook from that plan." No copy promises health, weight or diet results.
- Privacy (Legal L73 for the week menu, the L72 default for the rest): 127 doesn't take health information. The foods to leave out are just the foods, no reasons; who has an allergy and how serious it is stay on the paper intake. They stay in the run and its files, never in the Pipeline, and memory keeps dish ids only. `lib/privacy.js` keeps food restrictions and health details out of the Pipeline, memory and AI in the spine tools: a note about food or health in Your first 25 or Your next 25 is dropped before anyone is saved, a reply about food or health gets the pack's own answer with no AI call and only its stage saved, and the first cook day text keeps only the day and time. Free-text fields say "Nothing about their health." The kitchen intake is a blank paper form, and 127 never stores its answers.
- Cottage food: the sourced line uses Florida's law as the example (foods that need time or temperature control are not cottage foods) and always ends with "Check your city and state".
- One Pipeline row per client: the quote and the win are saved on their row in this pack, so `sales.quote` and `sales.invoice` run with `pipeline: false`. A stop or a "no thanks" gets no answer and goes to Lost.
- Gyms, yoga studios, churches and apartment buildings come from OpenStreetMap through the kit places finder when a city is set, with the OpenStreetMap credit in views and as the last CSV row. Offline, places are marked "(sample)" and never saved.
- The founding price is for honest feedback, never for a review. The member's promise covers what they control (a missed or late cook day makes the next one half price; a dish that isn't right comes off the bill), never how anyone's diet or health changes.
- `sample/meal.jpg` is a labeled sample illustration for the example run. Sample people, places, phone numbers and sites are made up.
