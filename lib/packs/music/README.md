# Music lessons pack

Start teaching music lessons on the 127 spine. Safety with minors comes first everywhere: parents book and pay for students under 18, lessons happen where a parent can see or hear, and nothing goes to students under 18 directly. Stories never name the student. No promise of exam results, audition spots, talent or a pace.

| Workflow | Title | Tier | Minutes | Credits | Ends at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `music.first_25` | Your first 25 | free | 20 | 2 | 15 people you know with a personal text and follow-up each, up to 10 local places (schools, churches, community centers, libraries, music stores) with an ask that respects their rules, the safety rules, a first call script, a dated plan and a CSV; everyone saved to the Pipeline |
| `music.offer` | Offer builder | builder | 10 | 1 | Price per 30-minute lesson from sourced ranges, first-students ladder (first 3, next 7, full), money model (block of 4, 45-minute lesson, every other week, a summer block), printable offer sheet (HTML, no scripts) |
| `music.reply` | Answer a reply | builder | 3 | 2 | What a parent's, a student's or a place's reply means, a short answer with one next step (price said once; background checks, missed lessons, age, adult beginners, no instrument yet, online or in person, price and busy seasons answered from the niche with sources; any ask for a promised result gets the work promised instead), the Pipeline row updated for Do my day; stop or no means lost |
| `music.quote` | Send a quote | builder | 5 | 3 | One lesson (30, 45 or 60 minutes), a block of 4 or a summer block of 6 at the founding, intro or full price (block discount and travel to their home as their own lines), a printable quote through `sales.quote`, then `sales.invoice` from the same lines when the family says yes |
| `music.new_student` | New student and lesson notes | builder | 5 | 3 | New student: the win logged (stage won, warm), booking, prep and reminder texts to the parent, a printable first lesson checklist with the safety rules, the invoice through `sales.invoice`. After each lesson: the note to the parent (or to an adult student) |
| `music.lesson_plan` | Lesson plan and practice log (signature) | builder | 5 | 1 | Instrument, level and age give the first 4 weeks (each lesson in timed parts, auto length from the sources), a printable practice log (4 weeks of 7 days, parent initials under 18) and a studio policy page to sign (tuition, missed lessons and makeups, stopping, recitals with the fee split from the costs, safety with minors), plus the text to the parent; saved on their Pipeline row |
| `music.keep` | Reviews and referrals | builder | 3 | 1 | A thank-you to the parent, the same honest review ask for every family (no reward), a referral ask and a referral card |
| `music.proof` | Show your work | builder | 5 | 3 | A short story from the parent's words with the exact permission ask (the student's name becomes "my student", a quote that names them is left out), then after a written yes a 1080 x 1350 card and caption; founding or free said plainly |
| `music.next_25` | Your next 25 | builder | 15 | 2 | The next 10 to 100 people and places, skipping everyone in the Pipeline (kit `leads.known`), saved with messages for Do my day, a dated CSV; offered as a Monday 7am repeat |
| `music.ready` | Look ready | builder | 15 | 9 | One set of facts for `site.build` (one-page site, with a music lesson headline), `local.gbp_kit` and `local.flyer`, so prices and contact details match; the founding offer goes in messages, the flyer keeps the free first call |

Tools: `music.list_first25` (code, 2), `music.build_offer` (1), `music.answer_reply` (ai write, 2), `music.plan_quote` (1), `music.log_student` (1), `music.write_lesson_note` (1), `music.plan_lessons` (1), `music.ask_reviews` (1), `music.draft_story` (1), `music.make_story_card` (2, render), `music.list_next25` (2), `music.ready_facts` (1). Composes `sales.quote`, `sales.invoice`, `site.build`, `local.gbp_kit`, `local.flyer`.

- Data: `data/niche.json` (crowd card, safety rules, partner types, texts, offer, ladder, money model, replies and objections, quote packages, the win checklist, story rules, ready, and `lesson_plan`: instruments, levels, weekly plans, lesson parts, practice minutes by age, studio policy wording, recital notes). Sources: `research/niches/music.json`, checked 2026-10-19.
- Shared code lives in `packs/_kit/common.js`. The pack's own code: `tools/lesson-plan.js` (the signature), `tools/lesson-note.js` and `lib/ready.js`, which adds the website headline and the line under it in music lesson words, because the site pack files music teachers under its tutoring profile.
- Lesson length on Auto follows Ensemble Schools and ArtMaster (30 minutes for anyone new, longer for older and more experienced students). The weekly plans, lesson parts and practice minutes are teaching judgment and say so on the page.
- Recitals: the fee per student is the costs divided by the students performing, rounded up. The notes on music copies (U.S. Copyright Office, Circular 21) and the live performance exemption (17 U.S.C. 110(4)) are for the member, with "ask the venue about its music performance licenses, and get legal advice when unsure".
- One Pipeline row per family: the quote, the win and the lesson plan are saved on their row in this pack, so `sales.quote` and `sales.invoice` run with `pipeline: false`. A stop or a "no thanks" gets no answer and goes to Lost.
- Schools, churches, community centers and libraries come from OpenStreetMap through the kit places finder, with the OpenStreetMap credit in views and as the last CSV row. Music stores aren't in the finder yet, so they show as places marked "(sample)" with a tip to add them by hand; samples are never saved to the Pipeline.
- The founding price is for honest feedback, never for a review. The member's promise is about the work (a missed or late lesson makes the next one free, a refund if the first isn't a fit).
