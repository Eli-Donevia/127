# Content engine (`content`)

Hooks from real searches, film-ready scripts, a posting calendar, designed carousels, content pillars, one long piece turned into a week of posts, and a ranking of the member's own posts, built from the member's own facts (business name, service, city or area, buyers, price, offer) and one call to action: a comment keyword plus what they get. Facts come from the brain; the inputs `keyword`, `magnet` (what they get) and `area` override it. Without a keyword the kind of business picks one (detailing: QUOTE, "I'll send you a price for your car").

## Workflows

| id | tier | steps (credits) | per run |
|---|---|---|---|
| `content.hooks` 30 hooks for your business | free | search_questions (1), write_hooks (6), pick 3, write_script x3 (6 each), shoot_sheet (1) | 26 |
| `content.scripts` Scripts for your next 5 videos | builder | search_questions, write_hooks (15, plus your own ideas on top), pick up to 5, write_script x5, shoot_sheet | 38 |
| `content.plan_week` Plan your week of posts | builder | search_questions, write_hooks (24), plan_posts (7 days), review captions, make_calendar, write_script x up to 5, shoot_sheet, make_carousel | about 52 |
| `content.plan_month` Plan your month of posts | builder | same with 30 days and 40 hooks | about 52 |
| `content.carousel` Make a carousel | builder | make_carousel (8) | 8 |
| `content.pillars` Set your content pillars (5 min) | builder | search_questions (1), pillars (2) | 3 |
| `content.repurpose` Turn one long piece into many (10 min) | builder | key_points (3), pick up to 7 points, repurpose (8) | 11 |
| `content.rank_posts` Rank your posts (5 min) | builder | rank_posts (2) | 2 |

## Tools (input, output, files)

- `content.search_questions` (code, read, 1 credit). In: `{ area? }`. Out: `{ questions: [{ q, kind, source, a? }], live, service, where }`. Live: Google and YouTube search suggestions (public autocomplete, about 13 requests, cached 7 days), cleaned of job, seller, brand and other-country queries and localized ("near me" becomes "in Austin"). Offline, or when nothing says what the member sells: the niche's common questions (`source: "starter"`), so a vague business never gets searches for "service".
- `content.write_hooks` (ai write, 6). In: `{ questions, count = 30 (10 to 40), own?, keyword?, magnet?, area? }`. Out: `{ hooks: [{ id, rank, hook, onScreen, format, formatLabel, pillar, why, score, source, topic }], keyword, ctaLine, files, ai }`. 17 formats, every pillar present, real-search hooks ranked up. AI only sharpens the top 10 wording. Files: `<name>-hooks.csv`, `<name>-hook-bank.html`.
- `content.write_script` (ai write, 6, `viewMany`). In: `{ hook (required), ask?, keyword?, magnet?, area? }`. Out: `{ script: { title, hookText, format, pillar, ask, seconds, hook{say,onScreen,shot}, intro, beats[{say,onScreen,shot}], close, caption, hashtags (5 max), keyword, shots, spoken, silent, tips }, ai }`.
- `content.shoot_sheet` (code, 1). In: `{ scripts, area? }`. Out: `{ files, count, seconds }`. Files: `<name>-shoot-sheet.html` (printable, one page per video), `<name>-teleprompter.txt`, `<name>-captions.txt`.
- `content.plan_posts` (code, 2). In: `{ hooks (required), days = 7, perWeek = 5, time = "18:00", start? }`. Out: `{ posts: [{ n, date, day, weekday, time, platform, format, pillar, pillarName, ask, hook, onScreen, caption, cta, filmBy, holiday, title }], filming, holidays, carousel: { type, title, date }, reels, asks, mix, start, end }`. Rhythm: give, give, give, ask (2 asks in every 8 posts); one carousel a week; one film day before each week; US holidays and shopping days with a note.
- `content.make_calendar` (code, 2). In: `{ posts (required), filming?, holidays?, area? }`. Out: `{ files, posts }`. Files: `<name>-content-calendar.csv` (Date, Day, Time, Platform, Format, Pillar, Hook, On-screen text, Caption, Call to action, Film by, Status, Notes), `<name>-post-reminders.ics` (each post plus film days; zipped by the kit until the Library takes .ics), `<name>-content-calendar.html`.
- `content.make_carousel` (code, 8, `wants: ['render']`, `lane: 'render'`). In: `{ type: mistakes|myths|tips|signs|steps|questions|compare|checklist, topic?, theme: bold|dark|light, color? (hex), keyword?, magnet?, area? }`. Out: `{ title, type, slides, caption, files, sheet, video, rendered }`. Rendered: one PNG per slide (1080x1350, 10 max), `-slides.zip`, `-all-slides.png` contact sheet, `-video.mp4` (9:16, 2.5 s a slide), `-caption.txt`. Without render: `-slides-svg.zip` and `-preview.html`.

- `content.pillars` (code, draft, 2). In: `{ questions?, perWeek = 5, topics? (one per line, 6 max), keyword?, magnet?, area? }`. Out: `{ pillars: [{ key, name, theme, what, why, posts4, often, ideas: [{ id, idea, onScreen, format, source, topic? }] }], rhythm: { perWeek, days, weeks }, topics, ideas, live, vague, ctaLine, page, csv }` (`vague`: nothing says what they sell, so the names and ideas fit any business and the page says how to fix that). The five pillars named for the business, how often each comes up in 4 weeks at this pace (the same give, give, give, ask order `content.plan_week` uses), and ideas from real searches, the niche and the member's topics. Files: `<name>-content-pillars.html`, `<name>-content-ideas.csv` (idea bank with a Status column). Remembers the pillar names and topics.
- `content.key_points` (ai write, read, 3). In: `{ text? (30,000 max), url? (a public page of theirs, read once with `web.fetchPage`), title? }`. Out: `{ title, summary, points: [{ id, title, body }], quotes, source: { kind: pasted|page|sample, url, words }, ai, sample }`. Headings become points (else list items, else the best sentence of each paragraph). AI may tighten the points only when most of their words are in the piece, and quotes must appear word for word, or the plain extraction is used. Empty input reads `sample/long-piece.txt` (a made-up detailing blog post) and is marked sample.
- `content.repurpose` (code, draft, 8, `wants: ['render']`, `lane: 'render'`). In: `{ points (required), title?, summary?, quotes?, source?, keyword?, magnet?, area? }`. Out: `{ title, slides, caption, posts: { instagram, linkedin, facebook, threads[], x }, scripts (3), email: { subjects, preview, body }, quotes, week, checks, zip, sheet, page, rendered, sample }`. Every line comes from the piece. Rendered: `<name>-<title>-slides.zip` (carousel PNGs at 1080x1350 plus quote cards) and `-preview.png`; without render: `-slides-svg.zip` with previews in the page. `-posts.html` holds the week plan, the caption, 3 video scripts (say, on screen, film), the LinkedIn, Facebook, Threads and X posts with their counts, the newsletter (ends with name, business, address line and unsubscribe line) and the quote cards. Any post over its app's limit stops the run (`checks`).
- `content.rank_posts` (code, read, 2). In: `{ file? (Library CSV, TSV or TXT), rows? (pasted sheet rows, 200,000 max), keyword?, area? }`. Out: `{ count, from, to, base, typicalViews, top (5), bottom (3), more, less, next, groups, caution, columns, dropped, source: file|paste|sample, page, csv, sample }`. Never fetches or scrapes: only the member's export or paste. Reads common export headers (Publish time, Description, Post type, Views, Reach, Reactions, Saves, Follows, Link clicks and more; never a combined "Comments and shares" column), K and M numbers, decimal commas, US and day-first dates, UTF-16 files; skips Total rows; keeps the newest 500 posts. Score: 100 is the typical post, half from views against the typical post, half from weighted actions per view (like 1, comment 2, share 3, save 3, follow 5, click 2) smoothed toward the usual rate. Groups (format, kind of post, hook style, day, time of day) need 2 or more posts; do more and do less never repeat the same posts, flag when a group all went up in one time slot, and asks are judged by comments and never put on the do-less list. Under 10 posts it says to read it as a hint. Files, dated so a rerun sits beside the last: `<name>-post-ranking-YYYY-MM-DD.html`, `<name>-ranked-posts-YYYY-MM-DD.csv`. Empty input ranks `sample/posts.csv` (16 made-up posts) and is marked sample.

## Data

- `data/platforms.json`: limits checked on Oct 19, 2026 with sources: Instagram caption 2,200 and 5 hashtags, LinkedIn 3,000, X 280 with links counted as 23, Threads 500 (developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/posts), CAN-SPAM address and opt-out, and the YouTube Studio export steps (support.google.com/youtube/answer/9717005). Other apps' export menus are not named, only that their insights or analytics pages export.
- `sample/long-piece.txt` and `sample/posts.csv` are made up for a sample detailing business and are only used when the member gives nothing.

## Real vs sample

Everything is built from the member's real facts; nothing is invented about them. An onboarding category in the brain's niche ("Local service", "Online business") names no business, so the member's own words in `what` or `offer` come first, and with none of them the copy leaves the service out instead of saying "your service" (and files are named for the business, never `your-service-...`). Niche know-how (21 kinds of business, `data/niches.json`) is general advice with no statistics. Questions are live when the network is on (`--live` run on Oct 19, 2026: 30 real questions for car detailing in Austin, see `content.hooks`), otherwise starter questions. Platform limits in `data/platforms.json` were checked on Oct 19, 2026 with sources (Instagram caption 2,200 and 5 hashtags, 10 slides via scheduling tools, 1080x1350). No keys needed; AI only polishes wording when available.
