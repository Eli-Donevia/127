# Tech help at home pack

Start tech help at home on the 127 spine: helping households and older adults with phones, computers, Wi-Fi, printers, TVs and accounts. Trust comes first, because many clients are older adults: the member never asks for, sees, writes down or keeps a password, helps in person with no remote access apps, never handles a client's money or gift cards, and nothing in a text, flyer or card rushes anyone.

| Workflow | Title | Tier | Minutes | Credits | Ends at |

Tools: `techhelp.list_first25` (code, 2), `techhelp.build_offer` (1), `techhelp.answer_reply` (ai write, 2), `techhelp.plan_quote` (1), `techhelp.log_client` (1), `techhelp.plan_visit` (1), `techhelp.make_scam_card` (1), `techhelp.write_visit_note` (1), `techhelp.ask_reviews` (1), `techhelp.draft_story` (1), `techhelp.make_story_card` (2, PNG card), `techhelp.list_next25` (2), `techhelp.ready_facts` (1). Composes `sales.quote`, `sales.invoice`, `site.build`, `local.gbp_kit`, `local.flyer`.

- Data: `data/niche.json` (crowd card, visit rules, partner types, texts, phone call script, offer, ladder, money model, replies and objections, quote packages, the new client checklist, story rules, ready) and `data/visit.json` (18 issue types with steps for each device family, each step with its source or marked as judgment, the password manager steps, the scam card and the note wording). Sources: `research/niches/techhelp.json`, checked 2026-10-19.
- Signature code: `tools/visit-kit.js`. The rest is the shared spine in `packs/_kit/common.js` (with tutoring, petcare, coaching and resale).
- Passwords: no tool asks for one. The plan says "they type, you look away", and the visit note leaves out any line that looks like a password, PIN, code or account number and says how many it left out. Phone numbers stay.
- The scam card names no business, no price and not the member's number. It carries only the FTC, IC3, the Justice Department's Elder Fraud Hotline and the AARP Fraud Watch Network Helpline (their own numbers and hours), plus blank lines for the people the client trusts.
- One Pipeline row per client: the quote and the win are saved on their row in this pack, so `sales.quote` and `sales.invoice` run with `pipeline: false`. A stop or a "no thanks" gets no answer and goes to Lost.
- Senior and community centers, libraries, churches and apartment buildings come from OpenStreetMap through the kit places finder when a city is set, with the OpenStreetMap credit in views and as the last CSV row. Phone repair shops aren't a finder category yet, so they show as places marked "(sample)" with a tip to add them by hand; samples are never saved to the Pipeline.
- The founding price is for honest feedback, never for a review. The member's promise covers what they control (a late or missed visit makes the next one free, a refund if the first visit isn't right), never that a scam can't happen.
