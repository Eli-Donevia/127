# Event planning pack

Start day-of event coordination on the 127 spine: weddings, birthdays, showers and small work events, run by one coordinator who works with the vendors the client books and pays. The member promises only what they control: the final timeline a week before, and being on site from the first vendor's setup to the last teardown (miss either one and they refund their fee). Nothing in the pack promises how the day turns out.

| Workflow | Title | Tier | Minutes | Credits | Ends at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `events.first_25` | Your first 25 | free | 20 | 2 | 15 people you know with a personal text and follow-up each, up to 10 venues, florists, bakeries, photographers and restaurants with a partner ask and an in-person line, the ground rules, a free planning call script, a dated plan and a CSV; everyone saved to the Pipeline |
| `events.offer` | Offer builder | builder | 10 | 1 | Price per event from sourced ranges, first-clients ladder (first 3, next 7, full), money model (free planning call, day-of, planning hours, rehearsal, party, partial planning), printable offer sheet |
| `events.reply` | Answer a reply | builder | 3 | 2 | What a client's or a place's reply means, a short answer with one next step (the venue's own coordinator, insurance, rain, commissions, cost and "it's one day" answered with sources; a place asking for a listing fee or pointing to its rules gets the partner answer), the Pipeline row updated for Do my day; stop or no means lost |
| `events.quote` | Send a quote | builder | 5 | 3 | Day-of coordination, party coordination, partial planning or a block of 4 planning hours at the founding, intro or full price (the rehearsal as an extra line), a printable quote through `sales.quote`, then `sales.invoice` with the same deposit when they say yes |
| `events.booking` | New booking and planning checklist | builder | 5 | 2 | The booking logged (stage won), booking, prep and day-before messages, a printable planning checklist from signing to event week, and the deposit invoice through `sales.invoice` |
| `events.timeline` | Day-of timeline (signature) | builder | 10 | 1 | Event type, start time and the vendor list give a printable timeline with one owner per row, the vendor contact sheet and the day-of checklist on one page (one sheet each when printed), a message to the client and to each vendor a week before, day-before texts, a vendor CSV and countdown reminders for the calendar; saved on the client's Pipeline row |
| `events.keep` | Reviews and referrals | builder | 3 | 1 | A thank-you, the same honest review ask for every client (no reward), a referral ask for friends with an event coming up and a referral card |
| `events.proof` | Show your work | builder | 5 | 3 | The client's words as a short story with the exact permission ask, then after a written yes a 1080 x 1350 card and caption; founding or free said plainly |
| `events.next_25` | Your next 25 | builder | 15 | 2 | The next 10 to 100 people and places, skipping everyone in the Pipeline, saved with messages for Do my day, a dated CSV; offered as a Monday 7am repeat |
| `events.ready` | Look ready | builder | 15 | 9 | One set of facts for `site.build` (with event coordination words on the page), `local.gbp_kit` and `local.flyer`, so prices and contact details match; the founding offer goes in messages, the flyer keeps the free planning call |

Tools: `events.list_first25` (code, 2), `events.build_offer` (1), `events.answer_reply` (ai write, 2), `events.plan_quote` (1), `events.log_booking` (1), `events.build_timeline` (1), `events.ask_reviews` (1), `events.draft_story` (1), `events.make_story_card` (2, render), `events.list_next25` (2), `events.ready_facts` (1). Composes `sales.quote`, `sales.invoice`, `site.build`, `local.gbp_kit`, `local.flyer`. Every workflow in one pass comes to 26 credits; the free first run is 2.

- Data: `data/niche.json` (crowd card, ground rules, partner types, texts, offer, ladder, money model, replies and objections, quote packages, the booking checklist, story rules, ready, and `timeline`: event kinds, the wedding order, party, shower and work event programs, vendor roles with their usual arrivals, the day-of checklist and the countdown). Sources: `research/niches/events.json`, checked 2026-10-19.
- Shared code lives in `packs/_kit/common.js`. The pack's own code: `tools/timeline.js` (the signature), `lib/steps.js`, which passes the deposit typed in the booking form and on the quote to both invoices, and `lib/ready.js`, which puts event coordination words on the website, because the site pack has no event coordination profile yet, and hands the member's promise to the Google profile kit.
- The timeline: wedding order and lengths follow Zola's sample reception timeline, wedding vendor arrivals Zola's sample weekend timeline, and the method (work from the fixed points, one owner per cue, arrive first, a copy for every vendor) EventPlanning.com. Party, shower and work event programs and any arrival no source gives are judgment and say so; every arrival the member didn't type prints "(confirm)". One flex row (dancing, games or the program) stretches or shrinks to fit the end time, and the page warns when the program runs past it, when a vendor arrives after their first cue or before the venue opens, when a vendor has no phone, and when the day runs longer than the hours booked.
- Google profile: the local pack's events group lists photographer categories, so this pack uses the general group. Pick "Event planner" as the primary category in Google; the kit asks for an event planner group (see the build report).
- One Pipeline row per client: the quote, the booking and the timeline are saved on their row in this pack, so `sales.quote` and `sales.invoice` run with `pipeline: false`. A stop or a "no thanks" gets no answer and goes to Lost.
- Venues, florists, bakeries, photographers and restaurants come from OpenStreetMap through the kit places finder when a city is set, with the OpenStreetMap credit in views and as the last CSV row. Offline, places are marked "(sample)" and never saved. Referral fees are disclosed in writing to the client and in any post, and the partner answer says the member doesn't pay or take them (with a check to send it only if true).
- Ground rules on every list and checklist: a written agreement for every booking, clients sign and pay vendors directly, insurance before the first event, never buy, carry or resell alcohol, and permits for parks and public spaces.
- The founding price is for honest feedback, never for a review. `sample/` holds a made-up business in Richmond, VA, ten made-up partners and the example inputs; nothing in it is a real business or person.
