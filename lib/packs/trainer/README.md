# Personal training pack

Start personal training on the 127 spine. Safety first: every client fills in the official PAR-Q+ from the PAR-Q+ Collaboration before any session, the signature tool makes a plan only on a Go, and nothing this pack drafts gives medical advice or promises results (no weight lost, no new shape).

| Workflow | Title | Tier | Minutes | Credits | Ends at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `trainer.first_25` | Your first 25 | free | 20 | 2 | 15 people you know with a personal text and follow-up each, up to 10 apartment buildings, yoga and pilates studios, physical therapy clinics (referrals only) and running stores with a partner ask and an in-person line, the safety rules, a free consultation script, an intro class plan, a running store script, a dated plan and a CSV; everyone saved to the Pipeline |
| `trainer.offer` | Offer builder | builder | 10 | 1 | Price per session from sourced ranges ($35 to $100), first-clients ladder (first 3, next 7, full), money model (free consultation, session, 4-week plan of 8, 30-minute session, training partner, small group seat), printable offer sheet with a promise the member controls |
| `trainer.reply` | Answer a reply | builder | 3 | 2 | What a reply means, a short answer with one next step (the PAR-Q+, a clearance or the health form gets talked through in person and never goes to the AI writer, pain or an injury goes to the PAR-Q+ and a professional, results get no promise, meal plans go to a registered dietitian, certified, insured, cost, time, apps; a clinic asking about fees hears "none either way"), the Pipeline row updated for Do my day |
| `trainer.quote` | Send a quote | builder | 5 | 3 | One session, a pack of 4, the 4-week plan of 8 or a small group seat at the founding, intro or full price (a training partner as an extra), a printable quote through `sales.quote`, then `sales.invoice` when they say yes |
| `trainer.won` | A client said yes | builder | 5 | 2 | The win logged (stage won, warm), booking, PAR-Q+ prep and reminder texts, a first session checklist (PAR-Q+, emergency contact, insurance, CPR and AED, the stop rule) and the invoice through `sales.invoice` |
| `trainer.intake_plan` | Intake and 4-week plan (signature) | builder | 10 | 1 | One word of the PAR-Q+ (Go, Hold or Not done yet, never its answers or why), the date it was filled in and the redo-by date the trainer types (12 months after the form, or 6 after a clearance; 127 never learns which kind of go): a printable intake sheet every time; on a Go, the common notes (start slowly, low to moderate effort, any clearance precautions) and a dated 4-week plan (sessions on the calendar, the session template, 7 moves at 3 levels, pace and stop rules, a session log); on a Hold, all three hold messages (short illness, check with your doctor, the ePARmed-X+ next step) for the trainer to pick from; Not done yet or past its redo-by date, the line asking for the PAR-Q+ first. All of it in two files private to the member; the run view reads the same for every result |
| `trainer.keep` | Reviews and referrals | builder | 3 | 1 | After the first four weeks: a thank-you, the same honest review ask for every client (no reward), a referral ask and a referral card |
| `trainer.proof` | Show your work | builder | 5 | 3 | The client's words as a short story with the exact permission ask (no photos, weights or measurements), then after a written yes a 1080 x 1350 card and caption; founding or free said plainly |
| `trainer.next_25` | Your next 25 | builder | 15 | 2 | The next 10 to 100 people and places (apartment buildings, yoga studios, physical therapy clinics, running stores), skipping everyone in the Pipeline, saved with messages for Do my day, a dated CSV; offered as a Monday 7am repeat |
| `trainer.ready` | Look ready | builder | 15 | 9 | One set of facts for `site.build`, `local.gbp_kit` and a flyer through `local.flyer`, so prices and contact details match; the flyer carries the free consultation, not the founding price |

Tools: `trainer.list_first25` (code, 2), `trainer.build_offer` (1), `trainer.answer_reply` (ai write, 2), `trainer.plan_quote` (1), `trainer.log_client` (1), `trainer.build_intake_plan` (1), `trainer.ask_reviews` (1), `trainer.draft_story` (1), `trainer.make_story_card` (2, render), `trainer.list_next25` (2), `trainer.ready_facts` (1). Composes `sales.quote`, `sales.invoice`, `site.build`, `local.gbp_kit`, `local.flyer`.

- Data: `data/niche.json`; sources in `research/niches/trainer.json`, checked 2026-10-19.
- Shared code lives in `packs/_kit/common.js` (with tutoring, petcare, coaching and resale). The signature tool is `tools/intake-plan.js`, with the dates from `lib/schedule.js`.
- The PAR-Q+ is named, never copied, retyped, edited or linked, and HQ takes no filled-in or uploaded form (Legal L72: no licence needed). Each message, view and file says "from the PAR-Q+ Collaboration" at its first mention, and the plan's "Built from" line says the Collaboration isn't connected with 127. eparmedx.com appears as plain text only, never in a client message.
- Data minimization (L72): the intake tool keeps Go, Hold or Not done yet, the form date and the typed redo-by date, asks no reason to wait, and its goals field takes goals only. Nothing about the result goes to the Pipeline, business memory, an AI tool or the run view an AI linked to 127 reads; the won, keep and story steps carry no health data, and a reply about the PAR-Q+ or a clearance gets a fixed in-person answer.
- The plan follows the PAR-Q+ (start slowly, valid 12 months), the ePARmed-X+ physician clearance (valid 6 months), the CDC talk test, the HHS Physical Activity Guidelines (strength work at least 2 days a week, 8 to 12 repetitions, 2 or 3 sets) and the ACE and NASM scope of practice. It is a starting template, not medical advice.
- Apartment buildings, yoga studios, physical therapy clinics (the kit `physio` category) and running stores (the kit `running` category) come from OpenStreetMap through the kit places finder when a city is set, with the OpenStreetMap credit in views and as the last CSV row. Offline, places are marked "(sample)" and never saved.
- Clinics are for referrals only, with no fees or gifts either way (the federal Anti-Kickback Statute covers clinics that bill Medicare or Medicaid; check your state).
- The founding price is for honest feedback, never for a review. The member's promise covers what they control: on time, a written plan for every session, the next session free if they're late or miss one, and a refund if the first paid session isn't a fit.
