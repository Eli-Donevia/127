# Home organizing pack

Start home organizing on the 127 spine. Trust first: a free 20-minute walk-through before any booking, the client's yes on every item, supplies bought only after the sort and the measuring, and photos only with a written okay.

| Workflow | Title | Tier | Minutes | Credits | Ends at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `organizing.first_25` | Your first 25 | free | 20 | 2 | 15 people you know with a personal text and follow-up each, up to 10 real estate offices, movers, senior living, self storage centers and interior designers with a partner ask and an in-person line, the client-first rules, a walk-through script, a dated plan and a CSV; everyone saved to the Pipeline |
| `organizing.offer` | Offer builder | builder | 10 | 1 | Price per hour from sourced ranges ($35 to $150 an hour), first-clients ladder (first 3, next 7, full), money model (walk-through, session, room package, supply shopping, video check-in, move or downsizing package), printable offer sheet |
| `organizing.reply` | Answer a reply | builder | 3 | 2 | What a person's or a place's reply means, a short answer with one next step (bins before the visit, photos and privacy, unsafe homes referred out, downsizing, insurance, cost, with sources), the Pipeline row updated for Do my day |
| `organizing.quote` | Send a quote | builder | 5 | 3 | A 3-hour visit, a room package (6 hours), a move or downsizing package (12 hours) or a video check-in at the founding, intro or full price (supply shopping as an extra line), a printable quote through `sales.quote`, then `sales.invoice` when they say yes |
| `organizing.room_plan` | Room plan and supply list (signature) | builder | 5 | 3 | Room, problem items in the client's words and hours booked give time per zone (walk-through and wrap-up included, a helper halves the clock), what moves to the next visit, a supply list priced from 49 dated IKEA, Home Depot and Office Depot listings, a donation and recycling checklist ("check your city" where rules vary), a before-and-after photo plan with a shot list, a printable room plan and the text for the day before. New client: the win logged, booking, prep and reminder texts, a first visit checklist and the invoice |
| `organizing.keep` | Reviews and referrals | builder | 3 | 1 | A thank-you, the same honest review ask for every client (no reward), a referral ask for friends and neighbors and a referral card |
| `organizing.proof` | Show your work | builder | 5 | 3 | The client's words as a short story with the exact permission ask (the after photo included in the ask), then after a written yes a 1080 x 1350 card with the photo and a caption; founding or free said plainly |
| `organizing.next_25` | Your next 25 | builder | 15 | 2 | The next 10 to 100 people and places, skipping everyone in the Pipeline, saved with messages for Do my day, a dated CSV; offered as a Monday 7am repeat |
| `organizing.ready` | Look ready | builder | 15 | 9 | One set of facts for `site.build`, `local.gbp_kit` and flyers through `local.flyer`, so prices and contact details match; the flyer offers the free walk-through |

Tools: `organizing.list_first25` (code, 2), `organizing.build_offer` (1), `organizing.answer_reply` (ai write, 2), `organizing.plan_quote` (1), `organizing.log_client` (1), `organizing.plan_room` (code, 1), `organizing.ask_reviews` (1), `organizing.draft_story` (1), `organizing.make_story_card` (2, render), `organizing.list_next25` (2), `organizing.ready_facts` (1). Composes `sales.quote`, `sales.invoice`, `site.build`, `local.gbp_kit`, `local.flyer`. No keys needed.

- Data: `data/niche.json` and `data/room-plan.json`; sources in `research/niches/organizing.json`, checked 2026-10-19. In the room plan, zones, keywords, minimum minutes, weights and supply counts are judgment calls (the file says so); room times, supply prices and disposal rules carry their sources. Retail prices are listings on 2026-10-19 and change, so the plan says to check them before buying.
- The room plan never says what a city allows: recycling, bulk pickup and hazardous waste lines say "check your city" (or county or state), with the EPA, FDA and IRS pages linked where a federal page applies.
- Shared code lives in `packs/_kit/common.js`; the signature tool is `tools/room-plan.js` with its math in `lib/plan.js` and the printable sheet in `lib/sheet.js` (HTML, no scripts, phone and print layouts).
- One Pipeline row per client: the quote, the win and the room plan are saved on their row in this pack, so `sales.quote` and `sales.invoice` run with `pipeline: false`.
- Real estate offices, senior living and senior centers come from OpenStreetMap through the kit places finder when a city is set, with the OpenStreetMap credit. Movers, self storage centers and interior designers aren't in the map search yet; offline, places are marked "(sample)" and never saved. `sample/after.jpg` is a labeled sample illustration and the places in `sample/partners.json` are made up (.example sites).
- The founding price is for honest feedback, never for a review. The member's promise covers what they control (late means that hour is free; a zone they don't like is redone within a week), never results. No claims that organizing reduces stress, saves money or improves health.
- The Google profile kit uses the general group (no primary category picked) because the local pack's cleaning group would file an organizer as house cleaning; the member picks the category in Google's own picker.
